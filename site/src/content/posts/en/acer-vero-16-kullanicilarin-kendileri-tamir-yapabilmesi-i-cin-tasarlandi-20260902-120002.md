While the repairability of electronic products becomes increasingly important, Acer is taking an important step in this regard. The new Vero 16 laptop model is specially designed for users to simply repair their devices. This approach is moving towards the goal of reducing the problem of e-waste and extending product life.

Let's look at the features that Acer Vero 16 provides ease of repair:

- **Adjustable port covers**: USB, HDMI and other ports can be easily removed and replaced.
- **Adjustable external hinge**: The screen hinge is designed as a separate component, it can change quickly in case of damage.
- **Car-free base panel**: Bottom case has a standard latch system, can be angled without any need for screwdriver.

This design philosophy is going to reverse traditional laptops. Most manufacturers restrict repairs to authorized services, keeping their products completely closed. Vero 16 gives users control and autonomy. A simple battery change or memory upgrade can now be done without need for professional help.

There is a growing movement in the field of sustainable technology and Acer is located in front of this movement. Vero 16 offers a more logical option for both consumers and the environment by combining quality and ease of reparation.
