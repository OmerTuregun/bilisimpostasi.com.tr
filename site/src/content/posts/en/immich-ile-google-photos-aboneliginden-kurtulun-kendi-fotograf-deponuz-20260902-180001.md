Cloud services like Google Photos are practical, but in the long-term storage fee build up and can create trouble losing full control of your data. **Immich** offers an open source solution to these problems and allows you to keep your personal photo archive completely in your own control.

The main advantages provided by Immich are:

- Fully local storage
- AI-supported search and automatic editing features like Google Photos Home
- By eliminating cloud subscription fees **long-term savings**
- Full privacy and control over your photos
- Smooth sinkronization on mobile and web interfaces

Immich is an ideal option for users who are familiar with server installation. Installation is quite simple thanks to docker container or direct installation options. Thanks to the features of artificial intelligence, you can automatically categorize your photos, make text search and find similar images.

This solution, especially for those who have high-resolution photo and video archives, saving the monthly or annual storage fees. It also means that your data is not monitored by any third party. For users who care about privacy and independence, Immich offers an option that brings change in cloud photo management.
