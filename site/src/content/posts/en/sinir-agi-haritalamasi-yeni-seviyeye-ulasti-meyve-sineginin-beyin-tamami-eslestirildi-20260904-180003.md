A breakthrough success was recorded in the field of neurobiology: scientists managed to map each neuron and link in the brain of the fruit fly. This important study was completed twice; the female is fly before, now the male is fly.

The main results of this success are:

- **Full nöral map**: All neurons in the brain of the fruit fly and synaptic connections between them were digitally recorded
-**: Structural and functional differences between male and female brains were revealed
- ** inspiration for intelligence**: This data set can be used in the training of machine learning models and brain simulations
- **Nörobiyology method**: It was performed using electron microscope and computer-supported image analysis technology

Complete mapping of the fruit fly brain is an important reference point in the field of neurotechnology. This brain containing about 100,000 neuronus offers valuable information that can deepen our understanding of more complex organisms. Differences between male and female brains show how biological differences are reflected in the nervous system level.

This study opened the way to map larger brain systems in the future and helps to understand the biological foundations of artificial intelligence development.
