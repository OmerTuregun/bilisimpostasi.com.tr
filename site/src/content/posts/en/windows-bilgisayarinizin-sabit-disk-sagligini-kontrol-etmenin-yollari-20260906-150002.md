The hard disk of your computer is one of the most critical components that all files and programs are stored. When problems in your disc are not noticed, data loss or system failure may result. Fortunately Windows offers tools that you can use to regularly check the status of your disk. Here are the main ways to protect your hard disk health:

- **Use Windows Disk Manager:** With built-in vehicle, you can display disk status and make basic error control
- **UseSMART technology:** The own diagnostic system of your hard drive warns potential failures in advance
– ** Try third-party software:** CrystalDiskInfo or Hard Disk Sentinel provide detailed reports of applications
- **Edited scan:** Windows built-in scanning feature can detect errors and bad sectors
-** Keep the temperature monitoring active:** Over heat shortens disk life and can cause malfunction

Hard disk problems often slow computer, strange sounds or file access problems. It is important to check immediately when you notice these symptoms. The S.M.A.R.T. system of Windows allows your disk to monitor itself and alerts if there is a possibility of trouble. So you can make backup on time or plan disk change.

Regular care is necessary for your hard drive to work smoothly for many years. Once you do the control process or twice a month, it will allow you to get rid of disk problems and protect your valuable data.
