Artificial intelligence researchers reveal a critical problem in information processing systems based on basic language models: the subject that the selected objects are reached correctly to the people who read at the end.

Let's consider the grounded language model works at three stages of pipelines:

- **Nesne selection stage**: The system defines the corresponding object from the data set
-**Metin rotation stage**: Passes related to selected object are taken
- **Cevap generation stage**: Answered to the question using collected evidence
- **Benchmark measurement problem**: Standard assessments control objects linked to data set, but this can be different with actual choice

The research team has supervised the system performance by examining 600 questions from a common question-taking task called HybridQA. Basic Finder: In most of the 1,463 dissolving record, which is viewed from the data set with the selected object, the handoff (el change) fails.

This problem shows that AI models live information loss in every step while they perform complex multi-stage tasks. Benchmark metrics also reveal that such quality problems are not fully reflected. The results of the research emphasize the necessity of improved audit mechanisms and more reliable handoff processes.
