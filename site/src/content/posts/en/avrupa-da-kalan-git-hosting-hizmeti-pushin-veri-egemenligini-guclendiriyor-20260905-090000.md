Pushin pays attention as a new hosting service that allows software development teams to safely host Git stores within the European limits. In the face of the requirements of local regulations such as increased data privacy concerns and GDPR, developers and companies are turning to data storage solutions in their geography.

The basic features of the service are:

- All data is stored in physical servers in European countries
- **GDPR compliant** user privacy guarantee with infrastructure
- Model offering flexible pricing options for open source projects and businesses
- More control and independent compared to services such as Central GitHub and GitLab Tags
- Ideal solution for corporate customers with data domination precision

The rise of regional hosting services such as Pushin reflects the increased awareness of data localization in the world of technology. Especially for companies that operate public institutions, financial sector and sensitive information, it creates an important trust factor that does not leave Europe if its data is not at all.

Such local solutions create a healthy competition environment against global technology platforms, but also contributes to the formation of a more responsive ecosystem in data privacy and regional legal legislation.
