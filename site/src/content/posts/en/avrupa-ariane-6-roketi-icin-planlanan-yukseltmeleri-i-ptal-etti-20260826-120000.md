An important change occurred in the space program of Europe. **European Space Agency (ESA) officially cancelled the planned upgrade projects of Ariane 6 rocket. This decision means that the current design of the rocket will have a longer-term development strategy.

Ariane 6 is a launch tool designed to replace Ariane 5 and aims to strengthen the position of Europe in the commercial satellite launch market. However, the agency has postponed various technological developments planned to be done in the production phase of this rocket and decided to redirect resources.

- The basic design and characteristics of Ariane 6 rocket will continue to stay unchanged
- Scheduled engine improvements and structural changes were canceled
- Agency develops alternative strategies for future space launch needs
- Cost control and budget management are shown as main factors behind this decision
- Ariane 6 will not be affected by the planned dates to be operational of the rocket

This cancellation reflects the technical and financial challenges facing Europe’s space industry** Alternatives to companies such as increased competition and SpaceX in the commercial space market seem to re-evaluate the strategy of ESA. The agency prefers to focus on timely completion of the existing Ariane 6 project and operational.

In the future, Europe is likely to investigate other technological ways to improve space launch capabilities. This decision can be assessed as a pragmatic approach and shows that Europe continues its importance to its space industry.
