It is a vital task to control hard disk health for MacBook users. If your storage device fails to one and hasn’t received a backup, it can become impossible to recover your files. Therefore, it is extremely important to monitor your disc health by making regular controls.

You can use the following methods to check the disk status on your MacBook:

- **Open the Disk Utility app** and check the status of S.M.A.R.T by selecting your hard drive
- **System Report** can see storage information and disc speed through**
- **You can make more detailed analysis using third-party software**
- **Time Machine backups** avoid data loss by keeping them regularly
-**Protect disc performance by keeping the storage area** at sufficient level

Disk health issues often manifest itself in the form of slowdowns, unexpected shutdowns or file access errors. If the disk health report gets worse, you need to start transferring your data to another storage.

By doing regular controls, you can extend the life of your MacBook’s storage device and protect your important files. Adopt a proactive approach to prevent data loss and immediately install a backup system.
