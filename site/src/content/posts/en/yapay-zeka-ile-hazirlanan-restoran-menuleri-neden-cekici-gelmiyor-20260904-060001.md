In order to improve restaurant owners menus, they often turn to generalizing AI tools. However, this solution seems problematic in terms of customer as operators. Because customers feel an incomplete in the description of the dishes offered, and they can perceive this situation deeply.

The menus created by artificial intelligence have a serious problem: ** Uniformity**. Generative artificial intelligence models produce text based on statistical possibilities, which leads to repeated similar expressions. In menu descriptions, this situation gives the following results:

- All food descriptions are written with similar structure and sentence patterns, so each dish loses its own character
- The adjectives used and idioms are drawn from the same pool, which creates monoton
- Customers feel discomfort from these stereotypes at the lower level of brain consciousness and do not care for eating
- The written explanations never carry out special and creative expression made by human writer
- Restaurant culture and local flavor stories are lost and going by AI

Human writers can reflect cultural nuances and local characteristics to menu descriptions by evaluating each meal context. Yet artificial intelligence cannot provide this depth. As a result, customers live a feeling that they do not say exactly why they want to eat. Technology may appear as a shortcut, but it compromises quality.

Restaurant menus need human creativity. While it’s good idea to use artificial intelligence as a supporting tool, it can lead businesses to losing their identity to be delivered to him completely.
