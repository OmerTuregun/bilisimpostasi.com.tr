Cocktail-making machines developed by Bartesian can be compared to automatic beverage dispensers like Keurig and Nespresso, but are designed specifically for alcoholic drinks. While this technology has made it possible to prepare professional-quality cocktails at home, the user base remains limited and the steep price tag puts it out of reach for many consumers.

When examining which demographics would actually benefit from these devices, commercial spaces such as game bars, restaurant chains, and cafes represent a more suitable target market. For individual consumers, however, these machines are more likely to remain luxury accessories rather than essential appliances.
