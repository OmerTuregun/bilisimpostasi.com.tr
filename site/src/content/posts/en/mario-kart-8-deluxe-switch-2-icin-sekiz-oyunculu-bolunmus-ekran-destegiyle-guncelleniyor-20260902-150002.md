Nintendo offered a free update to Mario Card 8 Deluxe game for Switch 2. This successful racing game released for Switch console nine years ago was one of the biggest expectations of players in the transition to the new generation hardwere and the company continues to invest in this game.

The most remarkable feature of the update contains:

- ** Local multiplayer experience with split screen support up to player** is significantly expanded
- Free update will become accessible for all Switch 2 owners
- The original game continues to be located in the center of Nintendo's product support strategy even after nine years
- Improved performance expectation with new hardwere optimizations
- Increases the charm of a classic option for local sofa multiplayer games

This move is an important example that shows how Nintendo supports old heads in new generation consoles. Mario Card 8 Deluxe was one of the most sold games of Switch and offer updated experience for Switch 2, a strategic decision by pulling new consoles while extending the life cycle of the game.

The split screen feature up to eight players will increase the attractiveness of the game especially in social gaming environments and family events. This update is a indicator of how rich the content will be available to players in the market of Switch 2.
