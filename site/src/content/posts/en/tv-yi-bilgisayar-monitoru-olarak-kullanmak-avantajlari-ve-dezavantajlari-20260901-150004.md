With the rapid development of technology, many users think to use their televisions as computer monitor for building home office or developing gaming experience. However, despite the great screen charm, the main differences between TV and monitor are critical issues that should be taken into account before evaluation.

The main aspects of using TV as monitor can be sorted as follows:

- **Great screen area** can increase productivity and gaming experience
- It is possible to get a wide image surface at lower cost
- More space is available for multitasking (multitasking)
- Flexibility is provided for entertainment and business combination
- impressive results are taken in displaying high resolution content

However, design and technical differences can lead to serious problems. Televisions have higher values than computer monitors in terms of input lag (gecikme). This situation is a serious handicap for games and professional applications that require quick response.

Furthermore, TVs are set for coloring and contrast values not considering computer monitors, but for film and array monitoring purposes. Clearness can be reduced in text and detailed charts, eye fatigue can increase. It is not as optimal as long-term use comfort monitors.

Although it seems to be tempting to use TV as monitors, a careful choice should be done by considering the purpose of use and budget. This solution, which can be suitable for casual use, is recommended to choose a real monitor for professional operation or competitive games.
