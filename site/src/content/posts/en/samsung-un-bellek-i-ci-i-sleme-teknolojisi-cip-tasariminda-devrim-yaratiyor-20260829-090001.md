Samsung introduced an innovative technology called Hot Chips 2026, one of the most prestigious conferences of semiconductor industry, **Processing-in-Memory (PIM)**. This technology is designed to solve a critical problem in traditional chip architecture: delay and energy loss experienced in data handling between memory and processor.

The advantages of PIM technology are as follows:

-** Reducing Data Movement**: Processing processes are done directly in memory, significantly lowering data traffic between processor and RAM
-**Energy Efficiency**: Processing in memory consumes much less power than traditional architecture
-**: System response speeds are increasing, as data migration expires
- **Paralel Processing**: Provides the capacity to perform simultaneous operations in memory cells

This approach is particularly vital for tasks requiring artificial intelligence applications, large data analysis and real-time operations. Samsung’s PIM technology has a wide range of usage potential from data centers to mobile devices.

The progress in this direction of technology industry is the indicator that future-generation processors will be faster, more efficient and stronger.
