Jio, managed by India’s richest man, makes a new move in the world of technology. The company aims to make old and non-date computers ready for artificial intelligence applications. This project is quite affordable price tag if it attracts attention: about 11 dollars for only two months.

The aim of this initiative and the advantages it offers are:

-** Artificial intelligence access with low cost**: 11 dollar price make artificial intelligence technology accessible for millions of people
- **Use of old hardware**: Already re-functional old computers located in homes or businesses Tags
- **Print-oriented solution**: conversion performed with software optimization instead of hardware upgrade
-**Wide potential market**: Millions of users in India can access artificial intelligence technology

This strategic movement reflects the increasing effort for the democraticization of artificial intelligence in emerging markets. Jio plans to provide users with artificial intelligence capabilities without having to buy expensive new computer with a software and service-based model. Thus, the company can gain millions of new users in its own ecosystem.

This approach reshapes the strategy of technology companies in developing economies. If Jio succeeds this move, similar applications can also promote other companies and accelerate artificial intelligence to reach a global audience.
