Jane Street publishes technical challenges periodically as one of the leading companies in finance technology. These challenges are designed to test the problem solving capabilities of programmers. A reverse engineering challenge, published in the last period, attracted the attention of the developer community and has revealed various solutions.

The main points of the source article are:

- **Challenge structure**: It is asked to understand and reveal the behavior of a secret algorithm or system from participants
-**Solution approach**: Determining hidden rules through systematic test and data analysis
- **Technical skills**: Problem decomposition, hypothesis development and verification ability implementation
- **Hacker News interaction**: 14 points on the publication and 5 reviews of the technical community
- **Shared lessons**: Error techniques and efficient problem solving strategies in the construction process Home

Reverse engineering challenges is an effective tool to improve analytical thinking skills of softwareers. For developers who want to work in the field of finance technology, such problems provide close experience to real world scenarios. Jane Street provides such open challenges and points the importance of access to the industry’s ability.

The softwareists who wonder the solution details can learn how to overcome the step-by-step monitored method and the obstacles encountered.
