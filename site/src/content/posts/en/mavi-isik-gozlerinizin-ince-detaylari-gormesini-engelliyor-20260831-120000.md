A new research conducted by Georgia University reveals that blue light emitted from digital devices has an unexpected negative effect on eye health. The findings of the study include important interests, especially for those who spend a long time across the screen.

- **Blue light reduces eye detail**: The research shows that exposure to blue light affects the ability to see fine details by affecting eye photoreceptors.
- **Digital devices risk factor**: Blue light emitted by smartphones, computers and tablets leads to problems especially in case of prolonged exposure.
- **The ability to distinguish contrast of the eye is weakened**: According to the working results, blue light limits the ability to distinguish between the opposite light levels of the eye.
- **Different light wavelength effect**: Blue light shows a more pronounced negative effect than the effects of other color spectrums.

Nowadays, most people have become unable to leave technological devices in everyday life. Our interaction with screens in many areas from business life to entertainment has increased. This research clearly shows that the cost of this digital load to our eyes.

The findings of the research emphasize the necessity of practical solutions such as limiting the display usage time or activating device settings using blue light filter. It is recommended to take these measures especially in the use of display at night hours.
