New tools are being developed to ensure artificial intelligence agents can be safely deployed in enterprise environments. OneCLI, part of Y Combinator's S26 batch, offers a unified agent platform for teams and provides each employee with their own secure, isolated personal AI agent.

The platform streamlines integration with popular services like GitHub, Gmail, Notion, and Dropbox, while incorporating mandatory human approval mechanisms for critical operations such as sending emails or deleting Linear tickets. This approach guarantees that businesses maintain complete control while ensuring automation security.

Similarly, Frugal Tokens is a tool designed to monitor the costs and usage of coding agents, simplifying the financial management of AI applications. Together, these tools create essential infrastructure for safely and transparently implementing artificial intelligence in the corporate world.
