Sonos opened doors to start a new period in audio technology. The products offered in September 2026 of the company combine smart sound solutions with artificial intelligence. This move aims to change the user experience of home audio systems basically.

Main features in Lansmanda:

- Artificial intelligence based audio recognition and adaptable sound control system Home
- Automatic set speakers to acoustic properties in home environment
- Seamless audio synchronization and management in multiple rooms Home
- Improved music discovery algorithms and personalized audio profiles Home
- Improved script system with natural language processing Home

New speakers analyze home environment using machine learning and dynamicly optimize sound quality. Users can create a more natural and intuitive interaction with AI assistant. The system adapts to the preferences of each user by learning in time.

This breakthrough of Sonos is considered an important improvement in smart home technology. Artificial intelligence-supported personalization will take the sound experience to the next level and allow users to create a smarter music environment in their homes.
