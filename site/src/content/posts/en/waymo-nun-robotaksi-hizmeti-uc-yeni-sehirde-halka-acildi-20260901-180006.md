The subsidiary Waymo of Alphabet reached a milestone in autonomous vehicle technology. The company’s robotic service has now become open to all the public in Denver, San Diego and Tampa. This expansion is an indication that non-human vehicle services become the main current transportation solution.

The basic features of this step of Waymo are:

- Denver, San Diego and Tampa begin to serve at the same time in three different regions
- Opening the public ring from users who have access list before the service
- Application of autonomous driving technology in wide diameter in real world conditions
- evidence of the effectiveness of AI and robotic systems in urban transportation
- For other companies, he managed to guide in the field of autonomous transportation  

Waymo is the result of research and development for years in artificial intelligence, sensor technology and security systems. The company has shown that your drive tools can work safely and reliably in everyday life. This technology has the potential to convert transportation habits in cities, not only vehicle ownership.

This expansion of Waymo shows that the autonomous vehicle industry is mature and spread to a wider geography in the near future.
