Alienware creates a new milestone in the field of player technology. The newest 25-inch monitor of the company breaks record in the industry by offering an awesome performance with a 560Hz refresh rate. This monitor aims at the maximum performance of players in competitive games and professional esports applications.

Advantages of 560Hz refresh rate:

- **Lower input lag**: 560 square views per second, minimizes latency between player and game
- **Free image experience**: Provides uninterrupted and fluent visual quality on quick motion scenes
- **Competitive advantage**: extra precision that delivers superiority to competitors in games requiring FPS and quick reflex Tags
- **25 inch optimal size**: Wide FoV (see area) provides quick pixel response time while maintaining its advantage
- **Professional standard**: E-sports tournaments and the level that can be preferred by professional players m

This monitor of Alienware shows that gaming monitors continue to force their limits. Reaching a refresh rate like 560Hz, an important success in both software optimization and hardware engineering. Especially for professional players playing in Counter- Counter, Valorant and similar comepetitive games, such devices can radically change the gaming experience.
