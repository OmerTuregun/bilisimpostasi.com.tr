Anker exhibited important innovations in the mobile device charging field in IFA 2026. This year, the range of products offered by the company brings practical solutions to the charging problems that users encounter in everyday life.

- **Display feature** has become standard in almost all new chargers, providing users with real-time battery status and charging speed information
- **Combo Charges** supports multiple devices at the same time, significantly reduce the number of users in travel and office environments
- **Portable power bank** products are more compact and lightweight with new designs
- Various connection options are offered for device compatibility wide, smartphones, tablets and laptops

This strategy of Anker focuses on simplifying users’ technology life. The addition of digital screens improves not only functionality but also user experience. This kind of combo solutions save time, especially for travelers and those who use a large number of devices in the workplace.

This product launches in IFA 2026 show how quickly the charging technology evolves. Anker has combined design and functionality by guiding industry.
