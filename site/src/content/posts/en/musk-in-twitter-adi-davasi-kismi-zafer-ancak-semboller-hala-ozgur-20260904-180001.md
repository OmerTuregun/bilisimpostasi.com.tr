Elon Musk’s X company resulted in the legal struggle on Twitter name, and the court partially decided to the Musk side. But the details of the decision reveal that success is not complete.

The basic findings of the decision made by the court are:

- X company shows itself as the old name of Twitter in the app stores, while other companies justify using Twitter brand
- The term "Tweet" is still available freely by everyone and remains outside the scope of brand protection
- Iconic bird logo is also similarly used by other applications or services
- The decision is focusing on the technical and legal definition of control on the brand name, allowing for wider use

This development is the latest of commercial brand disputes that appeared after Musk purchased Twitter in 2023 and renamed X. The decision of the court emphasizes the limits of brand protection: it can change a company name and try to check its old name, but for this, all associated terms and symbols can also not control alone.

Experts note that this decision can create a pacifier for future social media and technology companies. The commercial brand propositions the complexity of its cases and reveals the difficulty degree of brand management in digital age.
