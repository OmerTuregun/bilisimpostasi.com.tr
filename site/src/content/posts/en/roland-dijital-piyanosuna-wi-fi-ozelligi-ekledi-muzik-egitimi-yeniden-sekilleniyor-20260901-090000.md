While music instruments continue to adapt to digital age, Roland has taken an important step in the digital piano category. The new model of the company aims to completely change music education experience with built-in Wi-Fi technology.

New features can be sorted as follows:

- **Automatic software updates**: It can automatically download and install the latest software versions via piano internet connection
- **Training app integration**: Students can access professional teaching software directly from the piano
- **Real-time data synchronization**: It will be able to save the Pratiş progress data into the cloud and track from different devices
- **Remote training support**: Teachers will monitor the interaction of students with the piano and provide guidance

Roland’s technology demand behind this move lies in music training. The interest in digital educational tools after pandemi has increased, and this product is exactly the purpose of filling this cavity. Music offers connected devices, motivation and more personalized learning experience for teachers and students.

It is seen that music instruments in the future become part of the ecosystem of smart devices. Roland’s Wi-Fi integration can be a messenger of similar developments in the industry.
