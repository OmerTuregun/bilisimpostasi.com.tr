Microsoft partnered with TCL to drive Xbox gaming ecosystem directly to home living rooms. The Xbox app that will soon come to TCL smart TVs will allow players to access cloud games directly from TVs.

This new partnership offers significant advantages:

- Access to Xbox Cloud Gaming without Game Pass subscription is becoming possible
- TCL smart TVs can transfer Game Pass games and selected games through Xbox app
- The launch of the app will take place in the coming months
- Perfect timing is provided with the cloud game service** based on the **prescription that Microsoft will launch in November
- With subscription-free flexibility, users can play the game whenever they want

Microsoft’s strategy saves progress to make games more accessible. TCL partnership represents an important step of integration of Xbox Cloud Gaming into home entertainment systems. Especially the payment-per-use model offers an attractive alternative for players who are not subscribed to Game Pass.

This move can be the beginning of entering the main current houses of cloud gaming. The next months will show how much this technology is adopted by the consumer.
