TikTok significantly updates the comment section to increase the interaction on the platform. New features announced by the company will diversify the way users respond to content. Inspired by messaging apps, these updates aim to make TikTok a more social and interactive platform.

New review features include:

- **Sesli reviews** users can share audio recording instead of writing articles
- **Review surveys** allows viewers to create quick destructions about video
- **Photo gallery comments** can add multiple visual comments
- **Live photo comments** (Live Photo) lets you share dynamic visuals to support the product

These updates show TikTok’s capabilities from messaging platforms like Discord and WhatsApp to integrate into social network. Voice comments offer an attractive option for quick and natural communication in the mobile-first user base. Surveys will make it easier to start deeper conversations between constructors and audiences.

The platform converts the comment section to a multi-dimensional interaction area, exceeding one-way text with these new tools. Apart from the short video format, such participation options can ensure that TikTok does not separate from its competitors. Although not yet full distribution date is not certain, the features are expected to reach all users within a short time.
