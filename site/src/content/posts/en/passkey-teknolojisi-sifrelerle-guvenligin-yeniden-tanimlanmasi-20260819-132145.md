The digital security landscape is undergoing a significant transformation. Passkey technology is emerging as a new standard poised to replace passwords after decades of reliance on traditional authentication methods.

## What Are Passkeys?

Passkeys are cryptographic authentication mechanisms that utilize biometric data—such as fingerprint scanning and facial recognition—or device verification methods. Unlike conventional passwords, they are not simple passcodes that users need to remember.

## Why Are They More Secure?

Research demonstrates that passkeys offer several security advantages:

- **Resistant to Phishing Attacks**: Unlike passwords, passkeys are never directly entered into websites, rendering phishing attacks ineffective
- **Eliminates Weak Password Usage**: Human error is removed from the equation since users don't need to memorize anything
- **No Password Manager Required**: There's no need for additional software to store sensitive credentials

## The Transition Process

With guidance from sources like Engadget, users can begin transitioning to passkeys. The main steps include:

1. Creating accounts on supporting platforms
2. Enabling biometric options
3. Gradually replacing passwords with passkeys

Technology companies and websites are increasingly adding passkey support to their services. This trend appears destined to become a cornerstone of cybersecurity strategy in the years ahead.
