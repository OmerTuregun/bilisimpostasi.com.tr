Internet Archive is trying to live information heritage in digital environment by maintaining millions of books, audio files, video and web pages. But this massive task requires expensive server infrastructure and technology investment. The organization organizes a special donation campaign in September to overcome this financial difficulty.

The main features of the campaign are:

- **Three-folded matching**: Repetitive donations made in September will be increased by 3 layers by a nosever
- Server maintenance and electrical costs: systems that are constantly working in petabyte scale
- Long-term support: Monthly donation plans make it easier for the budget prediction of the institution by its structure
- No ads: Internet Archive’s services are entirely based on donations and grant support
- No access barriers: All archive resources are open to free and unlimited access worldwide

Technology companies can be turned off, websites can be removed and digital content can be lost. Independent organizations such as Internet Archive are served as a fuse against such information losses. The 24/7 operation of servers reveals the need for thousands of technicians and high energy costs, financial support of the organization.

September campaign encourages the donors to make the paid** which is supported. While one-time aids are important, monthly contributions allow the organization to create planned budget. Thanks to the matching mechanism, every donation is doubled in terms of strength and impact.

If we think how quickly the content can be lost, the presence of organizations like the Internet Archive becomes a critical requirement. The campaign aims to expand digital salvation awareness rather than simply collect donations.
