Decentralized social media platform Bluesky has become an increasingly attractive target for cyberattacks. Multiple large-scale DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) incidents throughout the year have exposed mounting threats to internet infrastructure security.

## Understanding DDoS Attacks

DDoS attacks work by flooding a target server with simultaneous requests from thousands or millions of sources, effectively crippling service availability. The attacks Bluesky has endured demonstrate the growing sophistication of network-based assaults.

## Why Bluesky Is Being Targeted

Following Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter, users seeking alternative social networks have gravitated toward Bluesky. The platform presents an attractive target because it:
- Offers decentralized architecture built on open protocols
- Maintains a rapidly growing user base
- Requires advanced technical infrastructure
- Faces potential attacks from various ideological groups

## Impact of These Attacks

Each attack causes:
- Service outages and downtime
- Questions about platform reliability
- Resource drain on technical teams
- Degraded user experience

## Defense Strategies

Bluesky and similar platforms must implement:
- Strengthened DDoS protection systems
- Redundant infrastructure design
- Real-time threat monitoring
- Optimized content delivery networks (CDN)

These incidents underscore that cybersecurity has become critical for social platforms to maintain operations and user trust.
