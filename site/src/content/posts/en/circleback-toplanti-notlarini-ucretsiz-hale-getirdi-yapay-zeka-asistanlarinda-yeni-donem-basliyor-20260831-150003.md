In the new era, where AI technology converts business efficiency, meeting management tools are becoming increasingly important. **Circleback** from these players added free layer by making a strategic move to increase user audience. Thus, a wider user audience can try the benefits of the product without paying.

The new strategy of Circleback contains these steps:

- Free tier is launched and the input barrier is removed
- Paid plans are offered with pricing starting from 14 dollars per month
- Artificial intelligence provides automatic note retention and summary during the meeting
- The product automates text removal from audio-based meetings
- Aiming to upgrade conversion rate with Freemium model

Tools like Circleback have become more critical with the increase of remote work and the prevalence of video conference use. These solutions supported by artificial intelligence are saved from tasks that are not related to the time of the professionals after meetings. Especially for sales, marketing and human resources teams, such automations significantly increase productivity.

The adoption of free tier model reflects the new trend in the commercial strategy of artificial intelligence tools. The companies choose the way to promote their products to large audience, then to pay for premium features. This step of Circleback aims to strengthen its position in the competitive market.
