have you noticed that your iPhone battery is running fast than expected? Although in some cases it is difficult to control battery loss, **which plays a critical role in relieving your phone up to the end of the day. It is actually very easy to diagnose this problem in Apple devices and can only be applied in a few steps.

You can use the following methods to check your battery consumption:

- Open Settings app and go to Battery option
- You can see which applications in Battery Health section consume more energy
- The trend analysis by comparing the data in the last 24 hours or last 10 days Home
- Think about removing or updating your applications that consume excessive battery
- Can also help disable application renewal in the background

* *Your battery health report** shows detailed how much energy each application uses in the screen and background. This information is invaluable to identify applications that consume the battery to the unnecessary place and take the necessary measures.

Regularly checking these settings is one of the simplest ways to optimize your iPhone experience. By taking battery management seriously, you can make your device work longer.
