Google has acquired the operational data from Spirit Airlines, which ceased operations due to financial difficulties, through a public auction. The purchase is intended to train and develop the company's artificial intelligence models.

Large datasets from the airline industry serve as valuable training material for machine learning models. Google's move demonstrates how technology companies are leveraging historical operational data from defunct carriers to advance their AI development initiatives.
