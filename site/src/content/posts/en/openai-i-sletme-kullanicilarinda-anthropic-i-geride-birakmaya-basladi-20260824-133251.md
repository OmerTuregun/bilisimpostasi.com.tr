The competition between the leaders of the artificial intelligence market is becoming more dynamic. After the release of the latest models of OpenAI, the market share between business users began to slip and OpenAI’s Anthropic has increased expectations.

New data reveals:

- Businesses are willing to quickly switch between different AI platforms when each new model version is released
- This surge is the source of concern for investors on how "fix" corporate AI spending
- OpenAI’s product updates trigger significant changes in customer preferences
- The competition between Anthropic and OpenAI is ran by the pursuit of users, especially large businesses

This indicates that the corporate AI market is not yet mature and the customer loyalty is weak. Businesses still want to test which platform is better than its technological advantages. In terms of investors, this volatility brings the question of how reliable the long-term income predictions are.

Even the first size is very quickly winning platforms, their competitors are seen to pass immediately when they offer a better solution. This dynamic reveals that AI companies should not only develop technology, but also develop customer loyalty strategies.
