Researchers have uncovered a critical security vulnerability in Microsoft Copilot. By exploiting a hidden input parameter, attackers could potentially compromise user accounts and steal sensitive information.

## Details of the Vulnerability

The hidden parameter discovered in the system allowed threat actors to redirect targeted users to suspicious websites. Users who clicked on these malicious links could have their passwords and sensitive data stolen without their knowledge.

## The Importance of Cybersecurity

This vulnerability highlights how critical security is for systems powered by artificial intelligence and large language models. Technology companies must conduct comprehensive security testing before releasing products to the market.

## Microsoft's Response

Following the disclosure of the vulnerability, Microsoft has begun working on a fix. Responsible reporting of such security findings plays a vital role in improving overall digital security and protecting users from potential attacks.
