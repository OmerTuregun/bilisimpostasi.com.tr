iRobot introduced the new flagship robot cleaner Roomba Max 875 Combo at IFA technology fair held in Berlin. **1.199 dollar device** is the first major product that has been exhibited after the company has passed the ownership of Picea Robotics, which saves melastane.

The unique feature of the new model will revolutionize carpet cleaning:

- When it is positioned on carpet, the body of the device creates vacuum to stimulate the carpet
- This technology provides stronger absorption by fully sitting on the texture of carpet
- Cleaning with Mop function is becoming more effective
- There is adaptive running system on different floor types
- Longer working time thanks to energy efficiency Home

The new iRobot managed by Picea Robotics has taken an important step in the robot cleaning industry with this product. This mechanism that seals itself to the carpet is positioned as the main difference that distinguishes itself from the products of the competitor. With the combination of technical innovation and practical design, Roomba Max 875 Combo offers an attractive option for consumers looking for high quality cleaning.

The product is now evaluated as a expected development between technology enthusiasts.
