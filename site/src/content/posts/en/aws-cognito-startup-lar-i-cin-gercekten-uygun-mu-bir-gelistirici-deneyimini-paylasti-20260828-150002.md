AWS Cognito attracts many businesses by offering cloud-based ID management services. However, a startup developer reveals that this service does not meet expectations and experience significant challenges.

The basic problems reported by the developer include:

- **The height of the push curve:** Cognito's complex configuration and documentation cause significant additional time loss for beginners
- **Non-flexible design:** The customization options are limited for non-standard authentication flows
- **No cost:** Difficult the budget planning of increased pricing after scale
-**Technical support failure:** Power of finding enough and quick solution when problems are encountered

Speed and cost efficiency in the Startup environment are critical factors. The convenience promise of Cognito is facing complexity and restrictions in real use. It seems more logical to choose simple and transparent alternatives especially for long-term projects.

This experience offers a valuable lesson that emphasizes the importance of actual user feedback instead of general promotions in the selection of technology.
