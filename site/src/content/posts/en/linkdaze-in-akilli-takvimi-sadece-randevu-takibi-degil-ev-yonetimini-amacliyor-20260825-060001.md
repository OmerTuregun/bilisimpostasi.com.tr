Digital calendar applications are often designed only for event planning and time management, Linkdaze aims to exceed these limits. The new platform offers a comprehensive solution structured to facilitate family life and separates the architect of the product from traditional calendar tools.

The main features of Linkdaze are:

- **Free AI meal planner**: Creating weekly or monthly meal plans using artificial intelligence technology
- All advanced functions are kept behind the payment wall and provide free access to users
- Collaboration tools for coordination of family members
- Management of households operations from home tasks to shopping lists Home
- Design philosophy beyond traditional calendar applications

Most smart calendar services on the market prefer freemium models that require payment for advanced features. The strategy of Linkdaze is adopting a different approach. The main functionality of the platform, including the AI meal planning feature, aims to provide access to a wide audience.

This approach makes the application more accessible especially for families who want to facilitate their home management. Linkdaze shows that calendar software can only be used to organize complex parties of everyday life, rather than meeting timing.
