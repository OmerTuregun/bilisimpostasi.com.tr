Meta officially brought the experimental app called Pocket that users can create their own games with the help of artificial intelligence to the United States. After the off-the-the-art beta tests, the meeting with a wider audience refers to a new era in the field of creative software development.

Pocket’s main features:

- **Vibe-code technology**: Users can write code with natural language commands or creative descriptions
- Interactive games without ever programming experience
- Features to share and explore created games with the community Home
- Design recommendations and improvements supported by artificial intelligence
- Optimized interface on user feedback collected from the Brazilian pilot program

The quietly executed testing process in Brazil allowed Meta to measure technology stability and user demand. After this experience, the development of the version for the US market is completed.

Pocket aims to reach millions of potential creatives by eliminating obstacles between AI and game development. This move of meta can be the beginning of the AI-based creative tools turn into main current applications.
