Artificial intelligence transforms agriculture in a rooted way. **FAIRY** system developed by Harbin Technology Institute manages full season operations for soybean production as a concrete example of this transformation. The system distributed in a real research farm represents an important progress in the field of agricultural decision making and automation.

The FAIRY system performs the following critical tasks:

- Land management from field preparation to excrete Home
- Optimization of October, irrigation and fertilization operations  u
- Monitoring and intervention for pest and disease control
- coordination of harvest, grain processing, drying and storage processes  u
- Maintaining extended-time workflows throughout the season

The system is decided at every stage using a data-oriented approach unlike traditional farming applications. This agentic architecture is designed to understand the complex nature of agricultural operations and provide real-time adaptation. In line with the information from physical sensors, the system optimizes almost every detail from the amount of fertilization to the irrigation program.

The success of FairY shows the practical value of agricultural technology and artificial intelligence integration. With the adoption of similar systems of more research farm, significant increases are expected in global product yield.
