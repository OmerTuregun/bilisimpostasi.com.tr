Ford is on track to complete a $2 billion factory modernization project dedicated to its new Fathom electric pickup truck, with plans to begin prototype production in the first quarter of 2027. The initiative underscores Ford's commitment to establishing a strong position in the competitive electric vehicle market, where the pickup segment represents one of North America's most profitable categories.

The transition to the prototype phase marks a critical milestone, demonstrating that Ford has achieved the necessary technological readiness and established the required manufacturing infrastructure. This substantial investment reflects the automotive industry's serious approach to the electrification trend reshaping the traditionally conventional pickup truck market.
