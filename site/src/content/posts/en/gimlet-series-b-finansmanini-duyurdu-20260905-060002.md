Gimlet announced that a startup in the field of AI and machine learning successfully completed Series B financing tour. This new investment points a significant milestone in the company’s product development and market access strategy.

New funds will enable the company to invest in the following areas:

- Expansion of research and development team
- Development of product portfolio and addition of new private
- Strengthening marketing and sales operations
- Improvement of infrastructure and technological capacity

The successful closure of Series B tour shows that the market demand and investor trust are strong for solutions offered by Gimlet. The company has managed to pay attention to the industry with the services that use artificial intelligence technologies.

This financing move is considered a strategic step to reach Gimlet’s global market to a more powerful position and improve competitiveness. In the future period, the company is expected to reach its targets by using the resources obtained efficiently.
