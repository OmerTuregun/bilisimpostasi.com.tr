Google moved Pics image editor to premium subscribe levels for its artificial intelligence-oriented services. This move is part of the company’s AI Pro and Ultra package owners with more advanced image editing tools. Pics app aims to modernize photo processing experience by providing users with artificial intelligence-supported editing capabilities.

Pics editor stands out with the following features:

- **AI-supported editing tools** with professional level image processing
- Additional access to Premium subscribe packages (AI Pro and Ultra)
- Advanced filter and conversion options Home
- Intelligent automatic healing properties Home
- Sync between devices thanks to cloud integration

This decision of Google is the acceleration indicator of the integration of artificial intelligence technology into consumer applications. The company that tries to expand its premium user base aims to art directors, content producers and photo enthusiasts through Pics. Thus Google continues to offer an integrated AI experience in its ecosystem.

The enhanced image editors now open access to the wider user group, meaning the democraticization of digital creativity tools. This step of Google shows that artificial intelligence technology has become a fundamental component of premium services.
