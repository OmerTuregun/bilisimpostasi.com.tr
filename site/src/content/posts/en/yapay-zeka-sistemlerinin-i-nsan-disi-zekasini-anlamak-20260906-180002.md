The article titled "An Alien Mind" published by OpenAI focuses on cognitive processes that are radically different from human consciousness of artificial intelligence systems. This article is about how language models work and how far their "falls" are from our understanding.

The main points discussed in the article include:

- Non-linear, parallel processing logic of artificial intelligence models is different from human brain’s consecutive, secretion structure
- Mathematical abstracts that language models use in text prediction and separation of their human meaning concept
- It is important to evaluate them as "out intelligence" while interacting with models.
- Problems in transparency and interpretation of decision-making processes of artificial intelligence systems

This review of OpenAI brings an important question to the technology glass: How to determine whether these systems have the genuine "anlama" capability? The article emphasizes the intellektual humility that angle AI researchers need to resist models.

This perspective is critical to adopt a healthier approach in AI development and distribution processes.
