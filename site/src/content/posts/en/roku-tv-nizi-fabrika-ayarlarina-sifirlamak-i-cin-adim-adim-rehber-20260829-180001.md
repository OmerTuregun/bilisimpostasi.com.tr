Do you have problems with Roku TV or want to sell the device and give it new owner? It is one of the most effective solutions to reset factory settings. This process removes all settings, apps and personal data by returning your TV to the original state.

The main ways to reset your Roku TV are:

- Reset from the Settings menu: Go to the settings of your TV, find the system option and select the factory reset option
- Use combination of power button: In some models, the power button resets the device to keep the printed for certain time
- Reset with Roku remote control: You can use Roku application on your phone or options available in physical control
- Use the reset button on the back side of the device: you can print it with a small needle and make hardware reset

Each method can be suitable for different Roku TV models. It is important that TV is closed during the reset process or has full battery power. After the process is completed, you will need to start TV from start and track the re-installing steps. Remember that network connection installation and log-in with your Roku account will be done again. Thanks to these simple steps, you can relive your Roku TV or transfer it securely.
