Nvidia announced that Arm-based RTX Spark processors will be integrated into laptops in October. Unlike Söyles, it was noted that only N1X chip will be presented in this month, the more powerful N1 version will be delayed by 2025.

Future laptops with RTX Spark N1X will be available in two different configurations:

- **First configuration** designed to offer performance at mini computer processor level
- **Top level equipped with Blackwell RTX GPU with N1X** 6144 CUDA core
- The top model will be 20 core Grace CPU
- Two different options are designed to serve various user needs
- Pricing and full technical specifications will be announced in the following weeks

This step is part of Nvidia's artificial intelligence and calculation performance make it more accessible for laptop computers. N1X will allow you to run high performance AI applications on mobile devices.

The October launch of the RTX Spark series can launch a new era in the laptop market and accelerate the widespreadization of artificial intelligence applications.
