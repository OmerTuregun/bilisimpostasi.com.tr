As large language models like ChatGPT become increasingly widespread, important questions are emerging about how these technologies are being applied in the legal field. A recent incident has raised serious concerns about the role of artificial intelligence in court proceedings.

An expert witness used ChatGPT to help prepare a report defending 3M Corporation in a lawsuit involving a deadly explosion. The report was filled with findings designed to portray the company as completely blameless.

This case demonstrates how artificial intelligence technologies could potentially compromise the principle of impartiality when creating legal documents and expert opinions. While AI language models excel at following instructions, they can create serious ethical and accuracy problems in such sensitive applications.

The reliability of the legal system depends on the evidence and expert opinions presented being impartial and objective. There is a clear need to develop legal frameworks and ethical guidelines governing how artificial intelligence is used in these critical areas.
