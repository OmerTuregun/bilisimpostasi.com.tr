Microsoft started a special program to surprise Xbox players in their birthday days. The purpose of this program is to value users and increase platform commitment. These gifts you can request for your birthday week are an interesting advantage that Xbox ecosystem offers.

The main features of the Birthday Gift Program are:

- You can make a special gift request from your Xbox account in your birthday week
- The content of presented gifts may vary and the game credit or in-game content can be
- To request gifts, you need to go to your account settings and verify the birth date
- The program can not be presented simultaneously in all regions, regional differences are available
- The gift you request may be limited term and may be used within a certain period

Xbox offers additional content regularly through Game Pass and Xbox Live Gold subscriptions, as well as the advantage it offers to players. Birthday gift program is part of this strategy and designed to improve user experience.

Those who want to take advantage of this gift program should perform all the steps required to ensure the safety of the account and save the date of birth accurately. So you can be ready to get a special surprise from the Xbox platform on your week every birthday.
