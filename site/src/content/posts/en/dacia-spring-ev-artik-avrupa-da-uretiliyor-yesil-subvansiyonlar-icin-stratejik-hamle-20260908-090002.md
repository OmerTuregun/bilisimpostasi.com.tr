Renault monitors a aggressive strategy in budget-friendly electric vehicle segment. The popular Spring EV model of Dacia brand is now the company, which started to produce in Europe, so it has the possibility of leveraging regional electric vehicle.

The main elements of this move of the company are:

- **17.900 euro price point** with entry-level EV market to be able to compete
- Ability to qualify for domestic subsides thanks to European production and lower the final price
- Updates highlighting athlete character in Spring's design
- Product strategy aimed at achieving consumers who want to achieve their green mobility goals
- efforts to compete in Western Europe with cheap EV models in Chinese market

Dacia Spring represents the process of adaptation to the electrical age of the traditional automobile industry. This decision of Renault clearly demonstrates the intention of keeping place in the European market by establishing cost-effective production facilities. While the demand for lower priced electric vehicles is increasing, models like Spring may be tempted to customers looking for price-performance balance.

This approach offers a pragmatic solution that brings together the economic viabilite concerns of the manufacturing industry with the expansion targets of the EU’s electrical tools.
