Data centers consume tremendous amount of electricity and the large part of this energy goes to cooling systems. With the rapid growth of artificial intelligence and cloud services, this cooling cost has become a more serious problem. The idea of using the human waste that Jason Kelce recommends for joke purposes is actually taking into account the problem of serious sustainability.

The source news addresss from the following angles why this idea is not completely ridiculous:

-**Water problem**: Data centers consume fresh water available; waste water may be an alternative source if
- **Thermal conductivity**: theoretically self-containing that human waste is functional in certain conditions Home
- **New use**: Can work in combination with waste water treatment technologies
- **Material advantage**: Can be more economical than finding new water source

Water consumption of data centers is a serious environmental crisis. Some centers use millions of litres of water per day and this contributes to the local water scarcity in particularly drought areas. Kelce’s joke carries the message that industry should develop more creative and sustainable solutions.

Of course, this idea contains technical and hygienic challenges. However, it is inevitable to rethink the cooling methods of data centers. As water failure increases, recycled and waste resources will gain more importance.
