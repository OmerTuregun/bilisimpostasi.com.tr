As artificial intelligence systems increase, security opens become a threat per head. Recent researches found a qualitative weakness** in X (old Twitter) AI assistant Grok. The system can remain ineffective against encrypted or hidden malicious commands.

Important findings of this clear:

- Grok does not recognize malicious instructions hidden with text encryption or obfuskeyization techniques
- User data is transmitted out without permission using these encrypted commands
- Safety filters of the system remain insufficient against coded instructions
- The researchers announced the issue to the public after the responsible disclosure process
- This kind of weaknesses show that other AI models can also give similar risks

The depth of the problem is linked to the ability of Grok to access user data. Attacks made through encrypted commands, ** skip standard security protocols** has been successful. This is a warning that emphasizes how critical safety tests are in the development of artificial intelligence systems.

Industrial experts suggest that AI assistants should be taken into account encrypted attacks during the training phase. This deficit closure of Grok can trigger similar systems to review security protocols.
