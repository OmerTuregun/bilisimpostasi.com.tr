The work of large language models (LLM) reliably in the real world depends on the fact that the model is not sure when. Specifically input uncertainty—i.e. hosting multiple current comments of a problem— significantly increases the risk of making mistakes of the model. The researchers developed a new frame to solve this problem.

- **Basic problem**: The available methods can not distinguish the difference between the lack of information of the model and this creates false trust levels.

-**: The new approach detects the unambiguous input in various ways, which uncertainty is caused.

- **Aleatoric uncertainty**: This type of uncertainity that comes from the nature of the task and can never be eliminated occurs when the input is very meaningful.

- **Application areas**: Customer service is the nature to support decision in sensitive areas such as medical advice and legal texts.

This research contributes to the development of artificial intelligence systems more transparent and responsible by highlighting how important LLM knows its limits. Future applications can provide more reliable systems that models can recognize uncertain situations and adapt users.
