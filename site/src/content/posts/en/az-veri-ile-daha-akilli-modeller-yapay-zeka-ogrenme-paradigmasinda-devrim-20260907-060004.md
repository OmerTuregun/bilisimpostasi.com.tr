The belief that dominates a long time in artificial intelligence research was that powerful language models should be trained on billions of tokens. But a new study published in arXiv questions this assumption and offers stunning findings. Researchers on Qwen3 model family have shown that models can develop advanced logical capabilities even using less data at**xtremal rate.

The main findings of the research are:

- On-policy diffuse (OPD) method radically differentiates from traditional methods by providing intensive teacher control for each token produced
- The models in the Qwen3 family offer more efficient approaches by proveing that token-intentional learning is not required
- Find out that the checker triggers the ability of paraxically stronger intelligence Tags
- Can be compared to less calculation at post-training phase

This discovery means a deep change in terms of economy and energy efficiency of artificial intelligence training. Large models necessarily get rid of the assumption required by large-scale data, open more accessible and sustainable AI development ways. The most appropriate learning strategies of the industrin are expected to start re-evaluating.
