An important move occurred in the investment world focused on artificial intelligence infrastructure. Adit Singh from the early-term investors of Cerebras, the famous venture capital fund was folded into Mayfield **substructure partner**. This assignment is remarkable in how strategic investments are shaped in the AI and semiconductor ecosystem.

The main areas to focus on the new role of Singh are:

- Integration of semiconductor technologies and their AI systems  u
- Cyber security solutions and infrastructure protection
- Physical AI applications and hardware development
- Technology investments in the Startup ecosystem  

Participating in Mayfield means recognition of Singh’s proven expertise in AI hardware. Being the first supporter of groundbreaking companies like Cerebras shows that technology investors focus on what areas and where they are strategic to come.

Such assignments indicate that infrastructure and hardware investments in the VC world have been increasingly important. The growth of artificial intelligence applications also increases demand for powerful physical infrastructure and security systems that support them. The participation of Singh clearly reveals Mayfield wants to be the pioneer of this trend.
