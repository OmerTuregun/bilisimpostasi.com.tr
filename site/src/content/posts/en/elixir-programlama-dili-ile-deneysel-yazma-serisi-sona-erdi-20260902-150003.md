The software developer Lucas Sifoni decided to terminate the discoveryal blog series, which started to examine Elixir programming language in-depth. Experiences and learned lessons acquired in the series led attention to constructive discussions in Hacker News by the technology community.

The main reasons behind the closure of the series are:

- **A comprehensive overview of the basic features of the Elixir language is completed** and the original targets of the series were met Tags
- Practical knowledge of functional programming paradigmn functioning has reached enough levels Tags
- The nature of the generated content and recycling received by the reader showed that the work is successful Tags
- The necessity of passing to new platforms or formats for in-depth review on advanced topics Tags

The articles in the series of Sifoni presented comprehensive technical analysis in Elixir ecosystem, Phoenix web framework and simultaneous programming. Nine comments area articles have been a catalyst to an active discussion in the developer community.

This type of experimental blog series is important to guide other developers and enthusiasts who want to advance on the same way by documenting the technical learning process. The decision of Sifoni is assessed as a natural result of a comprehensive learning cycle.
