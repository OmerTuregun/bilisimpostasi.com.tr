The framework was recognized by modular laptop technology, the company started to respond to an important problem from users in the last period. The BIOS update, published on the laptops of the Ryzen 7040 processor, has made the devices not fully working (brick) reports are intensively seen on social media.

** Master Points of the Driver:**

- devices are not opened during BIOS update and cannot access the POST (Power-On Self-Test) screen
- Affected users search back paths by contacting Framework support team
- The company accepted unexpected behaviors in some hardware configurations, despite testing updates
- Framework announced that it started working on the fix BIOS version to solve the problem
- Teams were moved to provide support and assistance to users affected

Although the framework is famous for its modular design, it has shown once again that it should pay attention to software updates. The company tries to determine the root of the problem by working directly with the customers who have feedback and reach the solution as soon as possible. Such problems in updates may affect the reputation of companies that support reparability and open hardware.

Technical support teams evaluate the status of recovering the affected devices and continue to provide alternative solutions to users.
