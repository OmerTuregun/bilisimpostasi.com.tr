Unacademy, one of the largest online educational platforms in India, turned into a stunning example of the challenges that cloud learning industry lives. The company was a new evidence of the consolidation of the edtech sector by selling competitor upGrad.

Unacademy co-founder and CEO Gaurav Munjal, revealing about sales, admitted the situation clearly:

- **206 million dollar sales price** with zirve valuation 94% decline* *
- The company has a value of $ 3.44 billion once

- Reshaping the educational market of artificial intelligence and automation technologies

The reasons behind the sale are complicated but open. During the Pandemi era, online educational platforms attracted huge investments, but growth rates were sharply dropped along with the normalization of post-pandemi. Moreover, artificial intelligence-based personalized learning tools began to create serious competition to traditional live courses models.

The sale of Unacademy shows the challenge of developing sustainable business models of technology companies, which can quickly erosion high valuations. EdTech sector will now continue to evilize towards more cost-effective solutions integrated with artificial intelligence.
