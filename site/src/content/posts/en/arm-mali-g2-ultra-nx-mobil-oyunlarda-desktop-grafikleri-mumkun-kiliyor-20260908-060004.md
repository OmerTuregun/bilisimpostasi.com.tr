Arm introduced a new GPU architecture prepared to change the future of mobile gaming and graphic processing. Mali G2-Ultra NX aims to achieve visual quality similar to desktop computers on smartphones by integrating artificial intelligence-based graphic technologies directly into the processor core.

Basic innovations of the new generation GPU:

- **AI-native graphic processing**: Artificial intelligence models provide real-time graphic optimization by working directly on the GPU
- Desktop-class gaming performance: Can render 3D scenes complex with higher resolution and frame rate
- Energy efficiency: Thanks to smart AI algorithms, battery consumption is kept under control
- Improved rail tracing: more realistic light and shadow effects with beam monitoring technology Home
- Game developer-friendly design: compatible architecture with existing gaming engines makes fast integration easier

The basic power of Mali G2-Ultra NX lies in placing artificial intelligence in the center of graphic processing. This approach allows GPU to distribute calculations wisely in places required and optimize unnecessary operations.

The mobile gaming sector will greatly benefit from this development. Game developers can now move their experiences for desktop platforms easier to mobile devices. Mali G2-Ultra NX points on the beginning of the proximity of mobile and desktop gaming experiences.
