Apple Wallet is the most practical way to organize your digital wallet. You can keep many things from credit cards to bank cards, flight tickets. However, managing all these cards takes place with a simple process, and it is easy to delete them when you need it.

Follow these steps to add cards to Apple Wallet:

- Open the Wallet app and click on the plus icon (+) at the top right corner of the screen
- Select the card type you want to add: credit/bank card, pass or ID
- Pull the photo of your card or manually enter the information
- Complete the verification process of Apple
- Your card is ready to use instantly after installation

Remove cards is also very simple. Once you have selected the card you want to open and delete, you will find the options available at the bottom of the screen. You can start the deletion by clicking on the menu icon in the upper right corner of the card.

This flexible system allows you to manage your cards dynamically. You can add cards you want to use temporarily and delete it quickly when your business is finished. These simple management features of Apple Wallet turn our smartphones into a real digital wallet.
