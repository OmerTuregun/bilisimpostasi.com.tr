Android Auto is one of the most practical features of your smart car systems. It allows you to control the phone from the vehicle screen instead of using phone for navigation, music and messages. However, the technology does not always work smoothly and sometimes can experience connection problems.

If Android Auto is not working, the basic control points you need to browse are:

- **Check Bluetooth connection**: Verify that your phone and vehicle system matches and matches Bluetooth. In some cases, the connection may be disconnected and rematched.
-**: Android Auto can also be connected with USB cable on some tools. Check whether the cable is properly installed and is not damaged.
-** Review phone settings**: Make sure your phone is off the USB Debugging mode; this feature can be roofed with Android Auto.
-**: Check out driver updates**: The software of the vehicle system can be outdated. Check new updates by manufacturer.

Most connection troubles are caused by communication interruption between devices. After doing these simple controls, the problem is solved in most cases.

Android Auto is alternative solutions to re-install the app, or install the cable/Bluetooth connection. If the problem persists, you can contact the technical support team of the vehicle manufacturer.
