This experiment offers a concrete example that shows how artificial intelligence can be used in the most personal areas of our lives.

Such experiments bring the question of how artificial intelligence is reliable in the field of medical consulting. Users report that artificial intelligence can offer valuable supplements, but also have limitations on personalized medical advice. ChatGPT can succeed in providing general information and creating motivation, but the necessity of professional consultant control in serious health issues continues.

Such applications show that artificial intelligence will expand its role in the mid-term role in health technology, but it should always be complemented by a human-oriented approach.
