Despite no monetary payments, there are important lessons in the startup ecosystem of this event. Rippling showed that a competitive product is launched immediately after the agreement and reacts.

This situation adapts founders and company leaders:

- **The cost of financial conflicts is not only money — time, there are high costs in terms of human resources and corporate creditibility.
- The dispute between the two-tech companies, affecting public and investor trust, and both sides have also praised.
- Immediately after the trial of Rippling, the competitor product removal shows that the tension at the beginning is not completely resolved.
- Although it is important to move fast in the Startup field, it may be more expensive to ignore legal risks.

After this event, the point that attracts attention is that avoiding conflicts or solving at the early stage is how valuable. The founders should take into account that the intellectual property disputes they encounter may be more rational in the long term of the choice of the path to the table, without going to the case. In this case, both sides have allocated ways with a unfinished relationship, which spreads insecure to the industry. Trust and reputation in the Startup environment is as valuable as money.
