In the field of medical image analysis, artificial intelligence plays an increasingly important role to improve the life of doctors and patients. The study presented in new research reveals a revolutionary approach to automatically identify disease findings. This technology is designed to solve the task of lesion segmentation, which plays a critical role in clinical diagnosis and treatment planning.

Current segmentation methods face two basic challenges:

- **Flush background noise**: unnecessary information on medical images makes it difficult to distinguish the lesion area of artificial intelligence models accurately.
- **Different lesion morphology**: It is impossible to look at different shapes and sizes of disease findings, addressing all situations of a single solution.
-**: Traditional encoder-code solvent architectures are robusting guidance based on information at early stage during operation.
-**Speciality restructuring**: The research is able to edit the features of the network more intelligently.

The new method addresses these issues using visual priority information. The system allows the model to make more accurate segmentation by providing helpful information in identifying lesion. Early guidance at the coder stage encourages the network to focus on the right features from the beginning. This approach offers significant improvements in complex medical images where traditional in-depth learning architecture is insufficient.

Research contributes to increasing segmentation accuracy in medical AI applications and hopes to develop more reliable diagnostic tools in clinical environments in the future.
