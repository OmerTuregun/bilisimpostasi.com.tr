Pokémon Champions offers a new opportunity to competitive players in the world: competitive battles on mobile devices. After the game was launched in April on Switch and Switch 2, it expanded with mobile version in June. But play at the top levels—for example, at the Pokémon World Championship—hala Nintendo Switch started to query accessibility because it is necessary.

Although inter-platform experience is almost identical, tournament participation rules are connected to a specific hardware of players:

- Beginners on mobile devices provide full access to all mechanicals and gaming content
- Switch and mobile versions are fully compatible across platforms
- Only Switch version formalization in tournament level competition Tags
- The hardware requirement prevents players while no technical reason
- This policy is assigned to the competition at the peak of mobile players

Pokémon Champions accelerate battles by offering a simulator-based system, hardware restriction looks clearly unnecessary. The game design and technological infrastructure does not make any difference between two platforms; it is understood that the decision is administrative. ** From the perspective of accessibility**, this creates an artificial barrier for those who want to participate in the championship.

According to the news of The Verge, this restriction is disappointed among players. Mobile games are expected to be attenuating this kind of divergence trend in the competitive sports environment while becoming strong enough nowadays.
