Philips Hue decided to add wall panels to the smart home lighting portfolio from September 3. However, instead of developing its own product, the company invented this category went to a strategic partnership path with Nanoleaf. This collaboration draws attention as an important development between two long-term competitors.

Users under new integration may:

- Check nanoleaf Hexagon, Triangle and Mini Triangle wall panels via Philips Hue application
- Manage these panels with their existing Hue devices
- Increase ecosystem width thanks to 40 dollar modules
- Use color synchronization and automations on all devices

This partnership is a milestone in the smart lighting market. Philips Hue users are now using the creative and modular designs of Nanoleaf, they will use the strong sides of both products in combination. Nanoleaf's colorful, geometric wall panels make it possible to further illuminate home environments when combined with the comprehensive ecosystem of Philips Hue.

This movement shows that the industry chooses the way of integration rather than competition. The result for consumers means a more flexible and comprehensive intelligent lighting experience.
