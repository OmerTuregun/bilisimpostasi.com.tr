The data center infrastructure sector is experiencing a technological breakthrough. Relativity Networks, which has secured $22 million in funding, is implementing hollow-core fiber—a rarely used technology—for data center applications.

## What is Hollow-Core Fiber?

Traditional fiber optic cables transmit light signals through glass or plastic. Hollow-core fiber, by contrast, features a structure with an empty channel running through its center. This design delivers several advantages:

- Light signals travel faster
- Data transmission occurs 30% more rapidly
- Latency is significantly reduced

## Significance for Data Centers

This development is critical for modern data center requirements:

**Cloud Computing and AI**: Machine learning models require massive data transfers. Faster transmission boosts computational efficiency.

**Geographically Distributed Systems**: When real-time synchronization is needed across global data centers, every millisecond counts.

**Financial Services**: High-frequency trading and real-time analytics demand the lowest possible latency.

## Industry Impact

While hollow-core fiber is not yet widespread, Relativity Networks' investment is paving the way for mainstream adoption of this technology. These types of innovations—which enhance user experience and reduce operational costs—are bringing fresh dynamics to the data center market.
