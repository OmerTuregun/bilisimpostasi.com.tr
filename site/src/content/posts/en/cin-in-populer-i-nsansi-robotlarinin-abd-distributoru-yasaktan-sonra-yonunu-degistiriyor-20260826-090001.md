The distributor of China’s most common Humanoid robots in the USA had to change the business model from the foundation while fighting increased commercial obstacles. International restrictions for technology triggered this movement that directly affects the robotic sector. The distributor company develops new strategies in search of finding a sustainable solution under these demanding conditions.

Seeing the changes to age:

- sales and distortion activities in the USA have been seriously affected
- Chinese origin human robots were included in commercial restriction lists
- The company started to focus on alternative markets and regions
- Technology export policies, stricter controls for robotic industry
- Distributor made adjustments on reposition and product lines

This pivot decision clearly shows the impact of global trade tensions and geopolitical factors in the advanced technology industry. Robotics and artificial intelligence products are subject to increased regulations due to its strategic technology category.

The distributor is trying to provide both business continuity with the new market strategy and adapt to the changing regulatory environment. This situation reveals once again that global technology distribution networks have a structure that can vary precisely and quickly.
