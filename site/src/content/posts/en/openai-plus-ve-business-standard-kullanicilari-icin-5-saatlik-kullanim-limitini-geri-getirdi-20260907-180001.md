OpenAI started to re-apply the **5-hour usage limit for Plus and Business Standard subscribers by making an important change in the use policy of the service**. This decision aims to open the confusing limit changes last week.

The main results of change are as follows:

- Plus and Business Standard users face a 5-hour monthly usage limit
- Limit resets (reset) value and practical benefit significantly decreased
- The reason for unconsistent limit behavior in a last week was announced
- It is understood that the restrictions applied to paid subscribers are re-reviewed

This step of OpenAI seems to be assigned to better manage user behavior and control service capacity. But the decision is not yet clear how to meet by paid subscribers. Users who subscribe to Plus and Business Standard plans are likely to be disappointed due to this limitation.

OpenAI will continue to be wondering how to set these policies in the future and what changes will do according to user reactions.
