Acer prepared a 7-inch model to expand the Predator Atlas series. This device increases the preferred range of players in the portable gaming market by offering a more compact alternative of 8 inch Predator Atlas 8.

The new model accommodates:

-**7 inch screen** with lighter and easy form factor
- The main features of Acer's Predator Atlas series are offered by protection
- More compact design than 8 inch version, easy portability in pocket and backpacks
- Player-oriented hardware and performance targets Home
- expected to be presented at a reasonable price point

Acer against increased competition in the market of portable gaming devices is trying to meet various needs of players with different size and price options. 7 inch screen is positioned as an ideal option for those looking for a more compact experience while maintaining the power of 8 inch model. This movement continues to offer standardized size options in portable gaming devices.

This strategic step of Acer evaluates the opportunity for players who prefer small form factor. Predator Atlas 7 will be positioned in the market as an option to compete with Steam Deck and similar devices.
