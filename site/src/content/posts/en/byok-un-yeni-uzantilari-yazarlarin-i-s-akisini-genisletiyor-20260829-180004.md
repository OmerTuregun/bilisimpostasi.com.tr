BYOK is a special device that focuses on writing art and is known for its simple design. This gadget, focusing on simple text editor, is seen as "lux" to the large audience, although it has a serious appeal for professionals dealing with intensive writing. However, the device aims to expand the user base by choosing an open way to develop.

The company started to provide additional features that meet different writing needs while maintaining the foundation structure of BYOK:

- Special extensions for scenario typing allow producers to prepare movies and series texts in the correct format
- Note receiving functionality ensures that data is kept regularly during research and planning stages
- Modules that support large literature projects help to manage complex structures of novel writers
- Customizable extension system allows each writer to choose vehicles suitable for their workflow
- Since the basic writing experience has never changed, I don’t compromise from minimalism Tags

The approach of the extension is intelligently solved by BYOK. While Ciist writers prefer minimalist experience, users with more complex needs can shape the device according to their requirements. This flexible design allows the niche product to expand market share.

Distraction-free writing devices continue to be an attractive alternative for writers who escape from increasingly technological complexity. This step of BYOK indicates how private-purpose gadgets can evolve.
