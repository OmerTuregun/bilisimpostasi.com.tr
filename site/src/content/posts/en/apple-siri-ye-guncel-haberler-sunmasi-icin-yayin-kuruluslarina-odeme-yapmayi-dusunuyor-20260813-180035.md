Apple is considering allocating a multibillion-dollar budget to compensate news outlets for supplying real-time news content to its Siri assistant. This move underscores the growing importance of protecting content creators' intellectual property rights as artificial intelligence technologies continue to advance.

By negotiating direct financial agreements with news publishers, Apple could introduce a new approach to resolving the copyright issues surrounding AI training data—a contentious topic in recent years. However, a critical question remains for the future of the media industry: will this model extend to all news organizations, or will it be limited to major players?
