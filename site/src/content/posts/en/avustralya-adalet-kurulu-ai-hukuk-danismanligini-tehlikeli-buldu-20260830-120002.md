Australia’s Fair Work Commission highlighted serious dangers of using artificial intelligence technology in law consulting. The institution has described the advice produced by AI systems **"the person is wrong"**, and such applications have been found risk in public terms.

Basic points on the topic:

- ** AI risks in the field of law**: Artificial intelligence is not able to provide reliable guidance on complex labor law Tags
- **Legal results**: The faulty AI advice can push their clients to make false legal decisions
- **User safety**: Individuals receive legal advice based on AI, may jeopardize legal rights
- **Editorian concern**: Official institutions began to alert the industry in audit Tags

This release shows that AI technology is not reliable in all areas. In particular, it is still at the very early stage to replace human professional in sensitive areas such as law, it can lead to severe mistakes in financial and legal aspects.

This warning in Australia is an important sign for other legal schemes and regulators in the world. At the stage of training and development of artificial intelligence systems **insani control mechanisms** are required.
