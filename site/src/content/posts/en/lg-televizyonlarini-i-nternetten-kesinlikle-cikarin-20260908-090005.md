A vulnerability that arises in the smart televisions of the LG brand, threatens millions of users. Technology experts call to cut the internet connection to all LG TV owners by specifying that they should be taken immediately.

This security issue has critical importance on:

- Provides remote access via connected devices
- Allows user data to be collected intensively
- TV's microphone and cameras can be abused
- Creates the threat of bounce to other devices of the home network
- Not yet published an official security patch

LG TVs are open to potential attacks via web-based applications and open ports. The vulnerability is located in the system that allows devices to be controlled via the internet, and this situation increases privacy concerns especially in houses.

Security researchers recommend to disconnect the device from Wi-Fi as a temporary solution or tighten firewall rules. The official description of LG and it is not clear when to publish it. It continues to be the safest option to close the internet connection for those who want to protect personal data.
