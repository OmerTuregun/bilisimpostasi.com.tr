the new concept iRobot exhibits at IFA Berlin technology fair **Roomba Duo** offers an interesting perspective on the future of cleaning robots. Combining two different robots in one system, this design aims to add a new size to home cleaning. We examined this dilemma closer designed with power and intelligence.

If we look at the basic features of Roomba Duo:

- Heavy-duty jobs of the main unit: Large robot with stronger structure realizes sweeping and washing tasks in large floor areas
- Special small robot for narrow spaces: More fine design that can be taken mechanically to clean difficult places like six, corners and fine spaces of tables m
- Ability to move together: Both robots significantly increase cleaning efficiency by walking at home at the same time
- Intelligent coordination: Robots can share certain tasks by recognizing each other

This concept can be a turn point in the design of cleaning robots. Instead of power and flexibility to a single robot, cooperation of different specialized robots offers a more effective solution. The coordinated work of two units can make home owners get more clean and faster results.

Roomba Duo, yet at concept level, shows how to develop automatic home cleaning. IRobot is preparing to implement a new approach based on modularity and cooperation in cleaning technology.
