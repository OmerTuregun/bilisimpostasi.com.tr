The prevalence of artificial intelligence practices in healthcare institutions reveals new security challenges. The existing security mechanisms can not disclose individual errors and close escapes caused by AI systems in clinical environments. The researchers developed a comprehensive assessment framework to fill this gap.

The study, which draws attention to existing systematic defects, emphasizes the following problems:

- **Total model monitoring** can identify performance changes but can not disclose individual error mechanisms
- Traditional patient safety reporting can record negative events, but AI can not analyze clinician and workflow interactions
- The root of errors is often hidden in complex relations of these three elements
- Available frames were not designed to predict when and why AI failures are affected Tags

The recommended methodology focuses on restructuring the fault and close escape events that occur in real world applications of medical AI systems. This approach provides in-depth understanding of how AI errors arise and what factors play role.

For the future of health technology, such detailed analysis frameworks will increase the confidence in AI systems and minimize the risks in clinical applications.
