Increased complexity of artificial intelligence practices increases the importance of safe data shopping between people and AI agents every day. AiSpace Client pays attention to this need as a platform with open source code.

The project offers the following basic features:

- With the temporary file sharing mechanism, artificial intelligence agents and users can transfer data in a secure environment
- Protection of data privacy thanks to the implementation of encryption and isolation protocols
- Ability to coordinate multiple agents in systems (multiagent systems)
- Community support and contribution with open source architecture accessible on GitHub

Such solutions become a key component of AI safety in corporate applications and research projects. AiSpace Client allows developers to create secure AI systems quickly.

The project has the potential to identify new standards in AI ecosystem in terms of file sharing and data security.
