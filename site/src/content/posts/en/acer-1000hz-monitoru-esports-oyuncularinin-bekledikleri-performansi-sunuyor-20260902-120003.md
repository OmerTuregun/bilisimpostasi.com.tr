Acer introduced new monitor series for esports and high performance gaming world. These products attract attention to technological innovations and radical characteristics, especially those who will attract the attention of comepetitive players.

Featured products of the new collection are:

- **24 inch 1000Hz monitor**: It allows players to capture even the smallest movements even by offering 1000Hz refresh rate, which is not similar to Esports scene
- **34 inch ultrawide model**: Optimized product for strategy games and efficiency work with wide viewing area
-**Dynamic renewal rates**: Different product series support variable refresh rates that adapt to GPU

1000Hz refresh rate offers a significantly different experience from traditional 360Hz and 240Hz monitors. Combining these features contributes to developing players’ response times by downloading input lag to minimum. Ultrawide option brings an alternative solution for users who want wide viewing angle.

This move of Acer shows how advanced performance race in the monitor market. Offering different options to Esports and casual players, these products combine advanced technology and practical design.
