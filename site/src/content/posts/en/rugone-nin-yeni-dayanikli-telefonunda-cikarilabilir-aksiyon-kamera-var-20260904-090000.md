RugOne offers a new concept in durable phone market. The newest model of the company has a waterproof action camera, designed to be removable from this camera device. This feature offers flexibility and versatility to users.

- Removable waterproof action camera technology optimized for video shooting under harsh conditions Tags
- Camera module, which can be easily separated from phone body, suitable for independent use
- Ideal for durable design, extrem sports and outdoor activities
- This innovative approach, officially introduced in IFA 2026, adds a new dimension to mobile photographer

When creating a niche segment targeting durable phones, industrial and adventure lovers, action cameras also have a separate market. The combined approach of RugOne aims to combine the powers of both categories in one device. This modular design allows users to continue using the phone normally when using the camera in other applications.

Market shows an increasing interest against waterproof and modular technologies. This strategy of RugOne is a leading example of functionality and flexibility in mobile device design. It is expected to be more prevalent of such innovative features in the next year.
