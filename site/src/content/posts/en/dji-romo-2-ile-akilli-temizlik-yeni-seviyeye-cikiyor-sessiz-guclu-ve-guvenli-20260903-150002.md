DJI, a pioneer in drone technology, is preparing to take a new step in the field of home cleaning. With the introduction of Romo 2 series, important innovations in the smart robovac market come. The company makes serious improvements in both performance and privacy issues in this new generation, taking lessons from the security open of the first generation.

Basic improvements offered by Romo 2 series are:

-**Two models option**: Transparent P2 and opaque A2 versions provide users with freedom in style preference
- **Engel climbing ability**: Small steps and obstacles can be overcome Tags
- **Ultra quiet operation**: Even night hours with lower noise level compared to previous model
- **Explosive obstacle detection**: new navigation technology that can detect even thin cables up to 2mm
- **Local data mode**: It is provided that users remain only on the device, cloud transfer is optional

The serious security openings of the first Romo have made the data privacy in this generation. The new local data processing mode ensures users stay on the map and movement data on the device. This approach is considered an important response to increasing concerns about the privacy of smart home devices.

DJI’s Romo 2 looks to set new standards in robotic cleaning devices both technical performance and data security. This product designed to meet the expectations of the market in this field attracts attention with improvements made in the smart home ecosystem.
