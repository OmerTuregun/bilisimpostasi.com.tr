FreeCORE TrueNAS Core continues to meet the needs of corporate and personal users data storage, as a storage management platform based on open source software architecture. This project provides a cost-effective solution by acting on the basis of the TrueNAS ecosystem with free software philosophy.

The main advantages of the project are:

- **Open source infrastructure** thanks to code review and customization
- Providing advanced storage features such as RAID, snapshots and deduplication
- Central data management and access control facilities on the network
- Low hardware dependence and work on various platforms
- Security improvements with active community support and regular updates Home

FreeCORE can be used from small businesses to medium-sized enterprise environments. This platform provides flexibility for system managers and IT professionals reveals a software-based approach to manage complex storage architecture.

With the increase of open source software preferences in storage solutions, the importance of projects such as FreeCORE is also increasing. For organizations who want to reduce cost in technology investments, such solutions are strategic value.
