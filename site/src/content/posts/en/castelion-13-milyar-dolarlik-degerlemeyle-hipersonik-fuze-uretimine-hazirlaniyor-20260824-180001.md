- Company **13 billion worth valueing**, indicator of investor trust and market potential in the sector
- Hypersonic technology was developed in a process of** much more cost compared to traditional producers

The company seems to be an important place in the market share by developing serial production capacity. This development opens doors to spread hypersonic technology not only economically.
