If you want to move your home movie experience to top levels, Dolby Atmos must be installed correctly. Many users complain that they cannot achieve optimal performance after purchasing the device. Yet, with a few basic adjustments and accurate positioning, you can experience the three-dimensional sound environment that this wonderful technology offers.

To get the best results from your Dolby Atmos soundbar:

- **Highly location**: Optimize the vertical sound spread by placing Soundbar on or below about 1-2 meters of TV
- **Central location**: Balance sound distribution by keeping your living space on a fixed platform
-**Wall Distance**: Protect the sound from crashing to walls by placing at least 30-60 cm from side surfaces
-**Wire management**:Contact lossless audio signal using the HDMI eARC connection
- **Settings menu**: Enable Atmos mode from the application and edit the audio profile by content

Another factor that is as important as placement is that the sound output has an unobstructed path. Not to put decorative objects on Soundbar, it ensures that the sound spread remains completely free. Also, remember that the device reveals the actual potential when playing compatible content. You will immediately feel the difference by opting atmos-supported movies on Netflix, Disney+ and other platform.

After the correct installation, you can experience the sound experience in cinema quality at the comfort of your home.
