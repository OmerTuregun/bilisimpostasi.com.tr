Google significantly increased the price of the popular streaming device Google TV Streamer. This device, which is 100 dollars before, will now be sold to 150 dollars. This 50% price rise is considered a remarkable move in the growing competitive environment in the streaming market.

- **Original price:**$100
- **New price:**150 dollars
-**Price increase rate:** 50%
- **Product:** Google TV Streamer, the basic component of Google’s home entertainment ecosystem Home
-**Etki:** means users increase the budget spent on streaming devices

This price rise can reflect the rise in the technology industry’s inflationary prints and component costs. According to market analyst circles, Google wants to highlight premium features and improved performance with this move.

However, competing products continue to be presented at more affordable prices. Consumers will have to evaluate between their own requirements and budget balance. Google TV Streamer will continue to be a strong alternative yet with features such as 4K support and smart TV integration.
