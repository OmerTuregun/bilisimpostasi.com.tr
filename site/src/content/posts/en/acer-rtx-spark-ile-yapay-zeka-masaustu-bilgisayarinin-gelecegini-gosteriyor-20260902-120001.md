Acer revealed the design of new generation desktop computers focused on artificial intelligence applications with RTX Spark project. This design concept offers solutions for AI-odlu calculation workloads by leading the latest graphics processor technology of NVIDIA.

RTX Spark project stands out with the following features:

- A new form factor that integrates NVIDIA’s latest RTX chip technology
- Artificial intelligence model operation and optimized design for data processing m
- Intelligent acceleration hardware architecture for desktop computers
- Targeted system to increase artificial intelligence performance in professional and consumer usage situations
- Configurable structure to future AI applications

Acer emphasizes the increasing importance of artificial intelligence in the desktop computer market with this design and shows that we are close to seeing computers with more AI features in the future. While RTX Spark is just a design concept, it clearly reveals the rapid development of the industry in the direction of AI hardware and investments in this field of large technology companies.

The continuation of such projects is the messenger of the future that artificial intelligence capabilities will be more integrated in the main current computers.
