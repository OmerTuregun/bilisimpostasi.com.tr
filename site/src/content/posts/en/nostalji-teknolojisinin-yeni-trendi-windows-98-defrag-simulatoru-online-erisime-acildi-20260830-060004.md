Digital nostalgia is becoming increasingly popular in the software development community. The new project called Defrag98.com offers Windows 98’s legendary Disc Defragmenter tool as a simulator running on the web browser. The project with 74 points in Hacker News and 39 comments has managed to attract the attention of retro tech enthusiasts.

**Project What Offers:**

- Single browser simulation of the original interface of Windows 98 Disc Defragmenter  u
- Time accuracy with realistic animations and color scheme
- Access without any software installation
- Nostalgic software experience in modern devices
- Educational and awareness purposes about retro computer culture

Such simulators are a documentary value on the basis of the software date. Users can experience computer management experience directly from the beginning of 2000s from the end of 1990s. Defrag98 is a simple yet effective concept that allows technology enthusiasts to re-exerve classic operating system era on mobile phones or computers.

The technology community’s interest in such projects proves how vibrant space is digital nostalgia market and retro simulation technology. Mini projects like Defrag98 express a cultural value in terms of protection of software heritage in the long run.
