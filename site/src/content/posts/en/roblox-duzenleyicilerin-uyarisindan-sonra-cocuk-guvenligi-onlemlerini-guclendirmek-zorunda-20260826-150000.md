Roblox has severe deficiencies in child protection mechanisms, although it is a popular platform that contains millions of young players. Regulatory institutions have identified **opens that enable adults to communicate directly with children by accessing the platform. This situation reveals a problem that has been a concern for a long time by parents and observers.

Among the undetected problems:

- Means that age verification systems are insufficient, adults can create or mimic child accounts
- Messaging filters fail in detection of disturbing behavior
- Access controls with children's profiles are missing or strong enough Tags
- Reporting mechanisms are unused or time-consuming by users
- Platform does not make enough technology investment to monitor and block suspicious activities Tags

Roblox Corporation has the obligation to improve their systems after these findings. The expectation of regulators is the strengthening of**automatic threat detection systems by taking advantage of AI and machine learning technologies. Platform management will have to take steps to combine artificial intelligence-based solutions with human moderators.

This indicates that the regulatory audit on online child safety increases. Technology companies will have to regularly prove their effectiveness even if they have protection mechanisms on their platforms.
