Dell stands for a new step in the laptop market. In the last time Apple’s MacBook Neo model has been observed similarities **Dell 14S**, designed as a product that aims to provide high performance at reasonable price point. According to Engadget’s review, this device has a strong competitor in its category.

The basic features of the product offer can be summarized as follows:

- Provides balance between 14 inch screen size and ease of use
- addresses alternative seekers using premium operating system with a reasonable price tag
- MacBook has similar hardware architecture compared to Neo and offers a different software experience
- Suitable for modern employees and students with light weight and fashionable design
- Supply competitive features in terms of battery life and processor performance Tags

This effort made in the Cheap laptop category shows that Dell wants to enter the premium segment market at a more accessible price. After the success of MacBook Neo, many technologies have started developing devices in similar features. Dell 14S is rated as a product that tries to open a solid place in this trend.
