Anker offers a new approach in smart home technology. MindBase’s device is designed as a versatile solution that combines the functions of central hub, network storage system and security officer.

The main features of Anker MindBase are:

-**Central Hub function**: It allows you to manage your smart home devices from a single platform
- **NAS integration**: Includes built-in network storage system for data storage and backup
- **AI security**: It protects your home with artificial intelligence-based security features
- **Multiple control**: Provides wide ecosystem support compatible with various smart devices

MindBase’s most remarkable addition, combining artificial intelligence technology with daily home management security systems. Thanks to this integration, the device not only transmit commands, but also detect abnormalities and adapt the user.

Anker is trying to stand out by developing such multifunctional solutions against increasing competition in the smart home market. Products like MindBase respond to the needs of consumers to reduce the number of devices and improve integration. This approach supported by artificial intelligence refers to the future of home automation.
