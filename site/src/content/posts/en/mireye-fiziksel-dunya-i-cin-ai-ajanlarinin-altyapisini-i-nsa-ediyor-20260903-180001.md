Mireye, the company of Y Combinator S26, develops an infrastructure platform to make smart decisions about physical places of artificial intelligence agents. The company managed by Founder Ansh combines all necessary components for location intelligence behind a single API and MCP server.

The platform offers basic services:

- Provides structural data, enriched information and signals about any location in the USA
- Quick integration with direct API access to answer questions
- Free trial package worth 5,000 credit (not card required)
- Skill modules and comprehensive documentation for agents

Developers can start their service by taking a free key at mireye.com. The platform shows practical applications in the demo video shared by the company builder. API documentation is open to access docs.mireye.ai.

Mireye has emerged with the goal of filling a gap that AI agents need to make location-based decisions in real world applications. The platform simplifies the process of artificial intelligence development by systeming the data and tools needed to support such decisions. Technology offers potential for many areas such as smart cities, logistics and location-based services.
