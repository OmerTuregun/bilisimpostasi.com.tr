Did you know that Apple has still existed iTunes service that revolutionizes in the Internet music world and continues to buy music? In the world of technology "iTunes died" the voices multiply, the iTunes Store continues its presence as a platform where it is possible to buy music in the Music app.

The rooted changes in Apple’s digital music strategy:

- **iTunes conversion**: While users can use the library, media management and purchasing functions of iTunes app in the old period, these tasks are shredded today.
-** Switch to music app**: Apple has integrated iTunes Store to music app while maintaining the purchase option, making the overall user experience based on streaming.
- **Apple Music dominan**: The focus point of the company has now been lost to the subscription-based Apple Music, but iTunes Store has remained an alternative to those who want to buy music.
- **A classic residue**: The presence of the iTunes Store represents the intersection point of the current streaming age with the digital heritage of Apple, once shaping the music industry.

iTunes completely replaced music suicide when introduced by Steve Jobs in 2003. However, with the prevalence of streaming services, iTunes Store has been pushed back, Apple has concentrated all energy and resources to subscription services such as Apple Music. Today, iTunes Store has become a more nostalgic reference.

In summary, iTunes continues its presence as a place where it is still possible to buy digital music if it is no longer a central platform. As Apple’s history and intersection point of the future, iTunes Store continues to have an interesting role in technology history.
