Cloud in a Bottle offers an innovative solution designed to reduce dependence on traditional cloud services and restore data control to users. The project aims to democratize the self-hosting experience, even those who are limited to technical information. This initiative scored 47 points in Hacker News was interested in the open source community.

The basic features of the project are as follows:

- **Simple installation process**: Non-technical users can also create their own cloud systems with step-by-step instructions
- **Privacy and data check**: Your data is not in third-party companies, but on the servers you manage
- **Open source infrastructure**: Project offers the opportunity to be developed based on transparency and community contribution principle
- **Material activity**: Provides a more economical alternative compared to expensive commercial cloud services

Self-hosting has been a field that requires technical expertise for many years. Cloud in a Bottle provides practical solution for individual users, small businesses and organizations that care about data privacy by removing this barrier.

The project approach is an important step in the technology community, which increases interest in the non-centered internet infrastructure and contributes to digital autonomy of individuals.
