The use of autonomous AI agents in corporate environments is rapidly expanding, but these technologies are trying to manage traditional human users and with control mechanisms designed for long term services. The new research published in arXiv reveals the incompatibleity between current management models and autonomous agents in three basic problems headers.

Basic problems are as follows:

- **Efemeral agents**: Providing AI agents (provisioning) quickly arises and disappears, making traditional identity and access management impossible
- **Previous actions**: Since the actions of agents are not programmed, they are selected by the model, what can be determined in advance
- **Dynamic discovery**: Agents are dynamically explored instead of being provided, which makes control and monitoring difficult

To solve these challenges, the research suggests five primitive principles to manage autonomous agents at work time**. These principles are designed to ensure that AI agents work safely, scalable and auditable in corporate environments. The proposed framework shows how traditional security and management mechanisms should be evolved in the era of artificial intelligence.

This research addresses practical challenges encountered in corporate distribution of rapidly developing autonomous AI technologies and offers an important management framework.
