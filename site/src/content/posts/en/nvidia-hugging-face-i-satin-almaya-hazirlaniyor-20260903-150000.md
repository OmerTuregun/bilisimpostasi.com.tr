Nvidia's Hugging Face is considered a significant improvement in the artificial intelligence industry. This strategic process aims to consolidate power consolidation in open source machine learning ecosystem and AI leadership of Nvidia.

Hugging Face played a central role in the development and sharing of transformer-based models in recent years. The platform provides access to millions of developers and researchers. With this purchase:

- Nvidia will have direct impact on open source AI communities
- The model center and tools of Hugging Face will be more stringent integration with Nvidia’s GPU infrastructure
- Developer ecosystem will provide easier access to optimized models on Nvidia chips
- Artificial intelligence distribution and management for corporate customers will become more rational
- Platform can be supplied with Nvidia's CUDA and other software frames

This process shows that Nvidia deepened its investment in the artificial intelligence software layer. Checking a platform like Hugging Face means Nvidia is at critical point in model development and distribution processes. Thus, the company creates a ecosystem that is connected to the entire stack from hardware to software.

Although this purchase reveals questions about the future of the open source AI community, it is seen as a consistent strategy for strengthening the hegemonia in the AI market in terms of Nvidia.
