Some users in the new C5 OLED TV series of LG encounter 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection problem. The factor causing this problem in the webOS 25 operating system seems to be associated with TV’s software zone settings. The good news is that the problem can be completely solved and everyone who follow the steps necessary can correct their device.

The basic features of the problem are:

- 5 GHz Wi-Fi tape is automatically disabled in some region settings
- Zone configuration directly affects the network performance of the device in the webOS 25 version
- The solution developed by open source community was shared on GitHub
- Method restores Wi-Fi access by changing the area setting of the device
- The process requires technical information but step by step can be applied

The solution consists of configuring the software zone setting of TV you have. This shows how impressive the region lock applications in LG devices. The developer community gives users the right to check by offering open source tools to solve similar problems.

Before receiving technical support, it is important to carefully read the instructions in the GitHub store. Such solutions help balance consumer rights with device manufacturers.
