AI automation startups continue to attract the attention of major technology companies. The acquisition of Relay by Google demonstrates how dynamics in this sector are rapidly evolving.

**Relay's Shutdown**

Relay, an AI automation startup that developed solutions for task automation, is being shut down as part of the acquisition. This closure represents more than just an economic shift—it signals consolidation in the AI market.

**What This Means for Google**

According to a statement by Relay's founder and CEO Jacob Bank, the integration will include:
- AI-powered automation features embedded directly within the Chrome browser
- Enhanced capabilities for users to work more efficiently with AI through Chrome
- A key component of Google's broader strategy to embed AI throughout the browser experience

**Industry Implications**

This move reflects several important trends:
- Major corporations are acquiring startups with effective AI automation technologies
- Web browsers are becoming central platforms for AI integration
- AI applications designed to boost user productivity are becoming essential components of everyday software tools

Google's acquisition demonstrates how AI automation is being integrated into mainstream applications and everyday tools.
