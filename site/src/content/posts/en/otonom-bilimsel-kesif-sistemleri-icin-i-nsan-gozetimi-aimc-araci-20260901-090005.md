Otonom scientific exploration systems develop rapidly and can produce numerous research ideas, experiments and articles with minimal human intervention. However, as these systems are strengthened, the results of scientists are effectively controlled are mandatory. Here at this point, AIMC is activated.

AIMC (AI Scientist Mission Control) is a visual analytical framework designed for human surveillance of autonomous scientific exploration systems. Basic functions include:

- Real-time monitoring of output quality and evaluation of the reliability of results
- Identifying repeating error models of the system and understanding the causes of these failures
- Monitoring how research progresses in time and visualization trends
- Selecting useful findings in the shortest term for review from thousands of automated discoveries
- Accelerate and improve the decision-making process of human researchers

The system provides an efficient way to find valuable content in large data volumes. Researchers may focus on direct results and quality indicators, rather than understanding complex algorithmic processes.

The tools like AIMC shape the future of scientific research by combining artificial intelligence and human expertise. Although machines can produce more jobs, the responsibility of selecting quality findings and verification will always remain in the hands of scientists.
