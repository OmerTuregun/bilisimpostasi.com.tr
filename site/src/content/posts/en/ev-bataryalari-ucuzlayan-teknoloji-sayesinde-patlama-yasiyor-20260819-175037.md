The home battery market has experienced dramatic price declines and rapid growth since its inception. Residential energy storage systems, once considered a luxury product, are now becoming affordable for middle-class homeowners.

**Technology Breakthrough**

Behind the price drop lies a specialized technology that significantly improves battery manufacturing or storage capacity. While the article doesn't specify the exact technology, it's clear that advances in lithium-ion battery efficiency or production scaling have played a major role.

**Market Players**

Tesla pioneered the residential battery market with its PowerWall product. Base Power and other emerging startups are challenging Tesla's dominant position. Increasing competition is driving prices even lower.

**Demand and Applications**

Home batteries are seeing demand across several use cases:
- Integration with solar panel systems
- Backup power during grid outages
- Taking advantage of dynamic energy pricing
- Pursuit of energy independence

**Future Outlook**

Parallel to falling prices, battery capacities are increasing and reliability is improving. If these trends continue, home batteries could become a standard part of residential infrastructure within the next decade.
