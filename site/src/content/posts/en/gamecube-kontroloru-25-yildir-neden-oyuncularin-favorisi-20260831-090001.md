Although GameCube controller is quite controversial as the design, today is still one of the favorite peripherals of the player community. This controller, which Nintendo met the revolutionary console in 2001, is a full unique in terms of shape and functionality.

If you need to examine the reasons behind the popularity of this controller:

- **25 years of support**: Nintendo still supports the controller officially today and is compatible with new launched products.
- **Internal game design**: The control scheme is perfectly adapted to certain types of games, especially for action and fighting games.
- **Physical design**: Asymmetric analog bar, index finger positioning and buton layout, provide users with natural ergonomics for hand.
- **Protectability**: Many controllers are still functional.
- **Nostalgia factor**: The group of players per generation sees the controller as part of their childhood.

When designing games, the features of the controller are taken into account and this has made the controller to offer an improved experience. Players playing on old Nintendo consoles today continue to prefer controller. Despite technology progress, a well-designed product is the best example why it can pass through the test of time.
