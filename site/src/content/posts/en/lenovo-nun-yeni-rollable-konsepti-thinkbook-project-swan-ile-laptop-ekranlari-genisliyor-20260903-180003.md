Lenovo continues to force limits in foldable and outable display technology. The new concept described in IFA 2026, **ThinkBook Project Swan is a business laptop with a display that can expand on the horizontal axis.

This innovative design highlights the basic features:

- 14 inch base screen can expand up to 17 inch according to user requirement
- Rollable display technology works in horizontal direction, offers a more controlled experience
- Work is designed for laptop category, professional users
- ThinkBook Plus Gen 6, which is the previous vertical expansion concept, cannot adopt a more moderate approach
- Lenovo’s rollable screen experience shows that it is home towards a practical version

Lenovo is not new to do with rollable display concepts. The company has offered various prototypes and concepts in past years, even the rollable screen model has made a successful output. Project Swan reveals with a form factor that addresses the actual needs of business users by taking advantage of this experience.

The horizontally expanding display keeps portability while providing wider working space in desktop environments. This is an ideal balance between the pool area and compactness for professionals working for long hours. Rollable technology offers a less fragile and user-friendly approach than foldable solutions.

Project Swan has yet been in concept phase, but it shows that Lenovo continues its innovative laptop design. It increases the expectation of similar technologies in future business laptops.
