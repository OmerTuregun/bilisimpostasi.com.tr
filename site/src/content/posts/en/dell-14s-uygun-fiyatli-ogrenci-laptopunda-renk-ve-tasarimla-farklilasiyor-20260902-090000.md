**Dell 14S**, announced in IFA 2026, pay attention to a new generation of affordable laptop designed for students. The device is inspired by MacBook Neo, and it is aimed to open it in the market with four different color options. The 14S positioned as a bridge between Dell’s premium XPS 13 series and entry-level models offers an attractive alternative to increased laptop prices.

Basic features of the device:

- configuration starting with 8GB RAM and 256GB storage space
- Four color options: Linen (beige), Powder Pink (pink), Washed Denim (blue) and Velvet Green (green)
- price strategy positioned under XPS 13
- Optimized design for student target audience m

This approach of Dell is particularly remarkable during the overall price rise of laptops. The market demands entry level and affordable options due to the rise of premium products prices, 14S aim to fill this gap. Colorful design options are seen as a strategic move to attract young users.

When student budgets are thought, the combination of 14S features offered is considered a reasonable choice. Although prepayments and official sales start dates are not yet disclosed, Dell believes that this series will close the gap in the market.
