The explosion in the AI training data market creates unprecedented opportunities for data-oriented startups. Companies such as Micro1 accelerate growth in this period and shape the future of the sector.

-**Micro1 reached 500 million dollars gross turnover level**: Startup stands out as high quality data provider required to train AI models
- The need for training of Devasa language models: ChatGPT, Claude and billions of lines data needed for other artificial intelligence systems
- Competition is growing: competitors such as Scale AI, Labelbox are speeded with similar tempo and share the market
- Increased importance of data quality: Companies demand processed, verified and labeled data rather than raw data
- Business model expands: Micro1 and its competitors offer consulting services in the collection and processing of data, beyond only providing data

This rapid growth shows how quickly the machine learning technology is, and how much data is dense in the process of training new models. Every major AI company needs millions of data points to create the most advanced models.

The next month is the expectation of this market to be faster. Training data startups will play critical role as the data infrastructure provider of AI revolution.
