Sony introduced party speakers named **ULT Tower** by taking a new step in music and audio technology. These devices have the ability to connect multiple speakers smoothly thanks to Bluetooth Auracast feature. Thus, users can experience consistent and strong sound experience in different areas.

The main features of ULT Tower speakers can be summarized as follows:

- Automatic synchronization with other ULT Tower devices with Auracast technology
- It is possible to play the same music part of multiple speakers simultaneously
- Design for use in batch environment in closed and outdoor areas Tags
- Equipped with strong bass and high volume Tags
- Offers practical installation, suitable for users’ minds

Auracast is a broadcast feature from Bluetooth 5.3, it can send audio to multiple recipients from one device. Sony’s benefit from this technology is revolutionary in the category of party speakers. The product offers the solution that music lovers and social events are expected.

ULT Tower shows Sony’s investment in audio technology and adopt new standards such as Auracast. This type of connection method in the future is expected to be standard in portable speakers.
