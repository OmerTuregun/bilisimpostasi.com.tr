Cross modal attention mechanism, known as the basic spine of artificial intelligence models forming audio and video, facing an unexpected problem: semantic information leakage. The new arXiv research offers a analytical framework called "dickat triangle" to examine this problem in-depth.

The basic findings of the research can be summarized as follows:

- **Dikkat Triangle Structure**: The text is systematically reviewed how three cross-eye edges connecting audio and video streams together.
- **Semantic Leak Issue**: In Multimodal diffffusion models, unpredictable information transfers occur during the notification pass from one fashion to another.
- **Information Routing**: How the semantic content during the production process is mapped between fashionities.
- **Model Internal Dynamics**: evidence that the attention mechanism is operated by uncontrolled means are presented.

This discovery offers important interests to make multimodal AI systems more reliable. Given that sound-video production models are becoming increasingly common for industrial applications, it has become a critical need to control such information leaks. The research emphasizes the necessity of our models to understand better "black boxes".
