A breakthrough step in computer science. The new system named ProofEvolve was developed to automate mathematical evidence of artificial intelligence models more rationally. The research blends classic logic systems with nerve networks and allows machines to learn from past studies and continuously improve themselves.

The main limitations of existing artificial intelligence-based evidence systems are:

-**I'm loss**: The information of solved problems is brought into the system with weight updates, but this process is expensive and inefficient
- Temporary warehouse usage: The verified intermediate steps are limited only by the current problem, without long-term learning
- Lack of self-importation: Systems are not kept and reuse past achievements
- The functionality of the information: The information gained can not be effectively applied in future problems

ProofEvolve addresses these issues. The system manages to store verified mathematical steps in a permanent knowledge base, using **neuro-sembocyd evolution** approach. In this way, machines become more effective in new problems by learning from their own evidence.

This technology can revolutionize scientific discoveries. When the evidence made by mathematicians take many years, optimized AI systems can find the right solution in seconds in millions of cases. Especially in basic science research, the potential of such systems is tremendous in evidence of complexorems.

As a result, systems such as ProofEvolve show that artificial intelligence can only be played by the head in scientific reasoning, beyond data processing.
