Stripe, a leading player in payment technology, is moving forward with its strategic expansion into artificial intelligence by acquiring AI gateway startup OpenRouter. According to reports, the acquisition is valued at over $7 billion.

OpenRouter, which describes itself as "Stripe for AI," provides gateway services that give users access to various large language models. The acquisition strengthens Stripe's position in the AI economy and aims to make it easier for businesses to integrate AI tools into their operations.

This type of consolidation activity indicates that the AI infrastructure market is maturing and that major technology companies are seeking to increase their control in this space. Behind Stripe's move lies the intention to combine payment processing with AI services, offering customers a more integrated solution.
