The Solar Roof project, where Elon Musk introduces excitement and millions of people expect, was officially terminated. According to the information disclosed by Electrek’s resources, the Tesla’s third-party installation network has now been reported that Solar Roof is no longer available for the order, and only the traditional solar panels will be supplied forward-looking. This decision represents an important breaking point in Tesla’s renewable energy strategy and reveals questions about the commercially transmitted challenges of human alternative energy technology.

Solar Roof concept was first presented to the public by Musk in 2016. The project has completely the same appearance with traditional roof tiles, but it is specially designed panels with solar cells. The intellectual appeal is that home owners with aesthetic concerns can produce clean energy without changing the appearance of their roofs. Musk and Tesla team promised that this technology will enable designers to integrate energy production without breaking the beauty of their home structures. This visionary approach created a wide curiosity and expectations in the world of technology and renewable energy sector.

However in the last eight years, Solar Roof project has been much more complex and cost-effective than expected to be realized. Production challenges, installation complexity and high price point prevent the mass adoption of the project. Traditional solar panels still meet the largest part of market demand with simpler installation process and more affordable price point. Tesla’s decision makers seems that the Solar Roof product isn’t commercially sustainable. The company officially demonstrates that it wants to monitor a diplomatic approach on how to end the project.

This termination decision reveals six important results in the technology industry. First, amphitched projects to combine design and functionality do not always provide commercial success. Secondly, even in renewable energy field, production efficiency and cost control are critical factors. The third can change the dynamics of the market for a long time passing to remove products from the first concept. Fourthly, even a big company like Tesla may fail in some attempts. The challenges related to the fifth, installation network and sales channels are just as important as product technology. Finally, the termination of Solar Roof shows that the development speed of renewable energy technology is still behind the commercial application.

Industry analyst and clean energy experts, this decision is not surprising, unlike an inevitable development. Although Solar Roof is a ambiguous project, the combination of roof materials with solar panels in real world applications has always been estimated. Electric production is a large number of variables, such as robustness of roof structure, precipitation, snow load, thermal expansion and long term durability, factors that need to be coordinated in system design. Furthermore, the reduction of solar panel efficiency over time occurs at a different speed of roof materials, which complexes maintenance and renovation planning.

While Solar Roof project closes, Tesla’s renewable energy strategy’s traditional solar panels and energy storage solutions are seen to restructure around products, especially like Powerwall. This pragmatic approach shows that Tesla wants to focus on real, scalable solutions in the field of energy technology.
