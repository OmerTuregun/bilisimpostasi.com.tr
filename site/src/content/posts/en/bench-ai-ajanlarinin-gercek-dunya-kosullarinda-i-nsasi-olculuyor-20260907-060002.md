Large language models (LLM) based software agents are now undertaken tasks such as customer service, dispute resolution and internal system management in production environments. However, while the construction of these agents is increasingly transferred to coding agents, the current assessment tools do not measure how successful the actual customer projects can be in complex conditions.

The  (2-Bench (hyper-tau-bench) that arises as a solution to this problem is a new benchmark platform that takes the process of constructing agent:

- In real world scenarios, AI systems evaluate their ability to create agents
- Measures the performance of coding agents in real conditions of customer projects
- Testing complex software architecture problems of models while producing
- Designed as the first comprehensive tool to evaluate the entire autonomous agent development process (end-to-end) Tags

The researchers noted that the existing benchmarks are not sufficiently reflecting the level of complexity expected in their actual customer engagement while measuring the structure blocks of AI. .2-Bench offers a more reliable measurement method for the development of ready-made agent software by filling this gap.

This study is an important step in the progress of AI agents towards industrial applications and is expected to become a basic reference point for the design of future agent building systems.
