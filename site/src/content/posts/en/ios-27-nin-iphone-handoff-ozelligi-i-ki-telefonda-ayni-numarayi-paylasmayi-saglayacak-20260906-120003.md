Apple’s iOS 27 operating system, which will be launched this month, brings users an important innovation that will be able to switch smoothly between two iPhones. ** This feature called iPhone Handoff** will allow you to use the same phone number on two devices and completely change the mobile experience.

The basic features of the feature are:

- The main device of the first by matching two iPhone is the ability to adjust the second as the auxiliary device
- Use the same phone number on both phones and switch them smoothly
- Xcode 27 shows installation and demo feature on Device Hub
- said briefly in the WWDC 2024 event but the details are now revealed
- Expectation of new iPhone models to be ready to use with the launch

Although the details on how iPhone Handoff will work exactly aren’t yet disclosed to the public, Apple’s demo clips are shown quite simple. This feature is thought to provide great convenience for professionals who want to separate business and personal life especially.

The world of technology estimates that iOS 27 will significantly improve the approach of Apple to offer a lot of devices thanks to this new feature. Further information is expected to be explained within the next week.
