In the academic publishing world, artificial intelligence is increasingly involved in more roles, a new problem arises: misleaders who have created major language models (LLM) arbitrator evaluations. HalluPeer developed by researchers aims to provide solutions to this critical problem.

Current problems can be summarized as follows:

- LLMs can produce fluent but unfounded claims, which makes the reliability of arbitrator reports
- Available illusion detection tools are worth appropriate for arbitrator assessments requiring long and technical academic articles Tags
- Increase in the scale of the academic arbitrator process increases dependence on automated systems but quality control mechanisms remain missing

HalluPeer is the first benchmarking set specially designed for scientific referee assessments. This tool helps consultant LLM systems verify their claims about scientific articles, against original research texts. Especially in complex and technical content, it aims to increase the reliability of assessments created by artificial intelligence.

This development is an important step in the ethical and reliable prevalence of AI use in the academic publishing ecosystem. Tools like HalluPeer can help reduce the operational burden of AI by maintaining the superior qualities of human arbitrators.
