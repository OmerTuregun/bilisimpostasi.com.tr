New research into social media algorithms has uncovered troubling findings about X's (formerly Twitter) algorithmic practices. The study demonstrates that X's algorithm systematically prioritizes what is known as "ragebait"—content specifically designed to provoke anger and outrage.

The findings are even more concerning when examined through a political lens. According to the research, the platform's algorithm disproportionately impacts Democratic users and related content, amplifying anger-inducing material more intensely for this audience compared to Republican-targeted ragebait.

This type of algorithmic bias highlights a fundamental flaw in designing social media platforms primarily to maximize engagement and user activity. The discovery strengthens ongoing debates about whether algorithms should operate with political neutrality, raising serious questions about the responsibility of major platforms to ensure fair treatment across the political spectrum.
