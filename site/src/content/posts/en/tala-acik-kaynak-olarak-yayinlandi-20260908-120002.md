The D2 project took a new step in the field of diagram and visualization. The vehicle named TALA has now opened access to everyone with its open source license. This decision will contribute to the growth and wider participation of the D2 community.

Benefits brought by TALA’s open source:

- The developer community can find and fix errors in the vehicle
- New feature recommendations and contributions can be performed easier
- It will allow D2 ecosystem to expand and grow faster
- More flexible use will be provided in software development and data visualization projects
- Long-term sustainability is not intended with open source model

TALA is a tool designed to improve the functionality of the D2 language. Open source publishing makes it possible to develop accelerated with the contribution process of the community seen in similar tools. The software is expected to become a valuable resource for architects, data analyst and technical documentation experts.

Such decisions highlight the importance of cooperation and sharing information in the field of technology. TALA’s open source provides users both freedom and trust.
