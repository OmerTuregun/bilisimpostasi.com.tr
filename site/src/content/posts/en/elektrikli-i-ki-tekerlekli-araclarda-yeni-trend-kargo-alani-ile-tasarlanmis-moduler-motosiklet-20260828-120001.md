Electric two-wheel vehicles increasingly focus on a point where more urban logistics settles in the center, the Belgian-based Any startup focuses on this market left empty: the need for cargo in real-world usage scenarios. LUV1 aims to fill this gap by combining modular electric motorcycle, portability and storage capacity.

The basic features of LUV1 are as follows:

-**120 litre cargo capacity**: It can carry various loads from free bags to work equipment
- Modular design: Configurable structure according to different usage conditions
- Electric drive system: Eco-friendly and low operating cost Home
- Animal transport support: safe transportation for pets
- City logistics focus: optimized for courier and delivery services Tags

While the city quickly changes, businesses and individual users are looking for more practical electrical solutions. Vehicles such as LUV1 are positioned as a more efficient way of logistics and daily urban transportation instead of replacing traditional cars. This approach offers a solution that the market is expected, considering the demand for electric vehicles with high cargo capacity.

The LUV1 project of Any shows successful bridge building between functionality and environmental responsibility in the electrical two-wheel vehicles industry. These innovative designs in future cities will play an important role in becoming more sustainable in transportation.
