Framework, known for its modular design philosophy, has unveiled a new generation of its 12-inch convertible laptop series. The second-generation model delivers significant hardware improvements, advancing both performance and accessibility for users.

**Key Features:**

The new Framework Laptop 12 is powered by Intel's latest Core Series 3 processors, delivering faster processing power and improved energy efficiency. The device offers expanded hardware customization options, giving users the freedom to build configurations tailored to their specific needs.

**Pricing and Availability:**

Framework has reduced the starting price from $799 on the previous generation to $699 for the new model—a $100 price cut that makes high-performance convertible laptops accessible to a wider audience. Pre-orders are already being accepted, with initial shipments set to begin soon.

**Linux Support:**

The company is also offering a pre-installed Linux option, marking an important move for open-source enthusiasts and developers. Thanks to its modular design, users can customize the Framework Laptop 12 to suit their needs and upgrade it in the future as requirements change.

These updates demonstrate Framework's continued commitment to a modular and user-centric approach to laptop design.
