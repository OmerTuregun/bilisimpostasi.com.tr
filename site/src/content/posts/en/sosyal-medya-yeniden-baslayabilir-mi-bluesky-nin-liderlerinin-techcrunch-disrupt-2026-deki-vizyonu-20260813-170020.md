Bluesky CEO Toni Schneider and COO Rose Wang presented their vision for a reimagined social media landscape at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, discussing whether platforms could essentially start from scratch. As content moderation challenges and user experience concerns mount on major platforms like X (formerly Twitter), decentralized alternatives such as Bluesky are gaining increased attention.

The discussion reflects a growing demand within the tech industry for a fundamental redesign of social media's underlying architecture. Decentralized platforms promise users more transparent, secure, and user-controlled experiences compared to traditional centralized social networks.

Bluesky's leadership highlighted the potential for these next-generation platforms to address longstanding issues that have plagued established social media companies, positioning decentralized solutions as a viable path forward for the future of social networking.
