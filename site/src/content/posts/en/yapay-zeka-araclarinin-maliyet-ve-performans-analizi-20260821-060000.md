As artificial intelligence-powered coding tools become increasingly prevalent, cost and performance monitoring have become critical concerns. Recent developments in this space highlight important trends in the industry.

## Frugal Tokens: Understanding Token Usage

Researchers have developed a tool called Frugal Tokens to help understand how much coding agents spend per session. This platform provides users with essential insights including:

- Total session costs
- Impact of cache misses on expenses
- Spending profiles across different users
- Overall usage patterns
- Estimated execution time
- Session overlap analysis

These tools represent a critical step in understanding the economics of AI services. By collecting data on the costs and efficiency of different AI models, developers can make more informed decisions about which tools to use.

## Performance and Consistency Issues

However, concerns remain on the performance front. Reports indicate that advanced models like Claude Opus 5.0 are experiencing "incoherence" issues at alarming levels. This suggests that model updates can sometimes produce unexpected side effects that impact output quality.

## Looking Forward

As AI tools continue to evolve, greater transparency around both cost and performance metrics becomes essential. Tools and reports like these contribute to bringing the industry to a more mature level of accountability and understanding.
