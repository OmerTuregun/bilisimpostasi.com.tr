Scientists have made significant progress in developing human identification systems using conventional WiFi signals. The technology can distinguish between individuals by analyzing radio waves emitted by WiFi networks.

**How the Technology Works**

The system analyzes how WiFi signals are affected and reflected by the human body to create unique biometric signatures. Each person's movement patterns, body structure, and other physical characteristics create distinct variations in how WiFi waves propagate. These differences can be used to authenticate identities.

**Potential Applications**

- **Security Systems**: Keyless authentication for home and office security
- **Healthcare Applications**: Remote patient monitoring and fall detection at home
- **Smart Home Technology**: Automatic device control and personalized settings
- **Access Control**: Secure entry to restricted areas

**Privacy and Security Concerns**

While the technology offers significant potential, it raises privacy concerns since it enables human tracking without cameras or other visible devices. Developing appropriate legal frameworks and ethical guidelines will be essential moving forward.
