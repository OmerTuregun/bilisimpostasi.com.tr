NASA offers a new way to join space research. In the process of the development of the Roman Space Telescope, a certain coordinate of the universe, which will observe the telescope by entering the public names, can "live". While this creative initiative increases public engagement in science and technology, it gives millions of people the opportunity to build a personal connection with cosmos.

The main features of the campaign are:

- You can have a unique pixel of the universe by entering any name
- A special coordinate assigned to the first viewing series of the Roman Telescope
- Participants indicate a place to be written with the names of the texture of the universe
- The campaign aims to mobilize public interest for space technology
- Telescope images will be public and will take place in these pixels

This initiative is an effective example of modern science communication. NASA provides access to a large audience, not only from the field of astronomical research. While the Roman Telescope is planning to review the regions of the universe that remains closed to the human eye, millions of people make it meaningful to be part of this discovery.

People who want to see their name in the eternality of the universe can apply yet. Such projects show how powerful technology and human connection can be.
