A security vulnerability discovered in Microsoft Copilot has raised alarms among cybersecurity researchers. The flaw involved a hidden input parameter that enabled hackers to compromise user passwords.

The attack mechanism was straightforward: when a user clicked on a malicious link, the hidden parameter granted attackers control over the system. Such vulnerabilities highlight critical gaps that companies can miss during security testing procedures.

Although Microsoft patched the vulnerability promptly, the incident underscores how critical security flaws can persist in systems even after thorough audits. Cybersecurity experts are urging organizations to conduct comprehensive reviews of their other products to identify similar vulnerabilities before threat actors can exploit them.
