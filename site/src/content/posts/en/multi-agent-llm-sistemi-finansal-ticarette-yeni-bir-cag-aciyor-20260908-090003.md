A new revolution in financial markets. **Multi-A**gents LLM Financial Trading Framework shared by Tauric Research opens doors to the development of commercial strategies by combining the power of large language models with collective decision making. This open source project aims to create smarter and adaptable trading systems with the work of artificial intelligence agents.

The basic features of the project are:

- **Multiple agent architecture** Thanks to its different strategies, AIs are able to analyse the market at the same time
- **Great language models can handle financial data with natural language using**
- **Open welding structure** offers an open ecosystem to the contribution of the developer community
- **Real-time processing** can react quickly to market changes with capacity
- From the first version that meets on GitHub, technology enthusiasts achieve their interest

While the project was yet in early stages, the Hacker News community attracted 14 points to the attention of the technology glass. While artificial intelligence and automation in the financial technology sector is becoming increasingly common, the potential of such frames is quite high.

Such multi-agent systems are moving towards becoming standard in future trading platforms. Due to its open source, researchers and entrepreneurs can perform experiments on this technology and develop new applications. The intersection of financial intelligence and collaborative AI systems seems to be more pronounced in the markets in the coming years.
