Amazon is accelerating the integration of AI assistants into more homes. Fire TV device owners can now access the enhanced conversational capabilities of Alexa Plus completely free of charge.

This decision reflects Amazon's broader objective to bring AI technology to more households. Alexa Plus delivers an artificial intelligence assistant capable of more fluid and natural conversations.

Within the smart home and connected devices ecosystem, such strategic moves have become a primary method for expanding user bases. Amazon's initiative represents a step toward improving accessibility to AI technology.
