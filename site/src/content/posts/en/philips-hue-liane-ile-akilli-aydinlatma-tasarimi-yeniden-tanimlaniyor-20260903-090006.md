Despite the rapid development of smart lighting technology, the market faced with limited products in terms of design for a long time. Although most colorful smart lamps and fixtures are suitable for young rooms, they create aesthetic contradictions in modern living areas. Philips Hue introduced a new line of products in IFA 2024 to solve this problem: **Liane rope lightings**.

Features of the new Liane series:

- Built with 17 different design variants, architectural-oriented concept Tags
- Combine classic aesthetic design with color changing capacity Home
- See compatible with modern and minimalist interior designs m
- Rope-shaped form factor offers alternative to traditional lighting fixtures
- The collection exhibited in IFA is open to purchase from now

This step of Philips shows that smart home technology is positioned at the intersection of luxury and functionality. Liane product group transforms intelligent lighting into a decorative element, especially for high consumers of design awareness. The rope-shaped structure provides versatile usage from wall decoration to ceiling-assembled systems.

This collection proves that smart lighting has become the central element of interior design, not only functionality. Philips Hue’s architectural-oriented approach seems to accelerate the adoption of smart light in all living areas.
