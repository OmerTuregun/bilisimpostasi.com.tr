On the date of technology industry, fine and light laptops have always been the goal of designers. Lenovo is a step away. The Project AeroBlade, which will be exhibited in IFA 2026, is switching to **solid state cooling technology, which completely eliminates the traditional fan cooling system.

Lenovo’s new concept laptop features are quite impressive:

- More thin body than 10mm with 14 inch screen
- 830 grams (1.8 pounds) weight
- Fully silent operation because there are no fans
- Frore AirJet solid-state cooling technology
- 30% thinner and 60% lighter than ThinkPad X1 Carbon

Looking at the ThinkPad X1 Carbon models of today, 14mm thickness and 1kg weight become standard. Project AeroBlade breaks these standards, **it can work without noise**. The biggest advantage of solid-state cooling technology is not to be mechanical parts, which increases durability and improves energy efficiency.

This concept carries important messages on the future of laptops. Silent, lightweight and portable computers are no longer just dreaming. This step of Lenovo can redirect other manufacturers to similar directions and create a new standard for portable devices.
