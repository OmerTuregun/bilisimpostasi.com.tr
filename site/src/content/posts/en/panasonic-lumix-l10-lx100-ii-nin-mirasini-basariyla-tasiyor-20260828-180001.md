Panasonic presented the new model to replace LX100 II in the compact camera segment. Lumix L10 successfully carries out the legacy of the leading model in terms of design and performance. Engadget’s review reveals some questions at the price point, while taking the style and technical ability of this camera.

The highlights of Lumix L10 are:

-**Faster focus and capture with improved speed performance**
- High quality optical system in compact body
- Proper image quality for professional users
- Styleish design and ergonomic structure
- Pretty high price tag

The camera offers a more dynamic performance compared to LX100 II. It works more effective especially in capturing fast moving objects, and image processing algorithms are improved. However, Engadget discusses that these improvements are not fully corresponding to the price level offered.

Lumix L10 can be an attractive option for photographers looking for a stylingish and portable compact camera. However, budget conscious users have thoroughly evaluated their alternatives.
