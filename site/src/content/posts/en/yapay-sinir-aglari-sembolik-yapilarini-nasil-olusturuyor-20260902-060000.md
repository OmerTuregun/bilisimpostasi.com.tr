Understanding how AI networks work, continues to be one of the basic questions of artificial intelligence research. A new study released in arXiv examines symbolic structures that appear spontaneously in the educational process of these networks.

The findings that attract attention to the research may be collected under the following titles:

- Nervous networks create symbolic representations that are not designed consciously during training
- hierarchical and organized structures appear in the final layers of networks
- These symbolic structures contain pronounced motifs that represent the concepts of model learned
- Research helps explain how deep learning models perform similar processes to abstract thinking
- Results open new ways to improve interpretation of AI systems

Such research lights out what’s happening in AI systems that are seen as black box. The research team examines the foundations of this automatic learning mechanism, following how complex representations are formed in the layers of networks.

The most important aspect of the findings is the emergence of symbolic structures **emerjan properties** (common, non-designed properties). This shows how artificial intelligence systems can create organized structures without direct intervention. The results of the research can be interpreted and contribute to the development of reliable AI systems.
