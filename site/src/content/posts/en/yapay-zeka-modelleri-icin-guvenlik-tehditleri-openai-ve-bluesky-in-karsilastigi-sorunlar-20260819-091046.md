Artificial intelligence systems have become prime targets for increasingly sophisticated cyber attacks. Recent incidents at OpenAI and Bluesky underscore the growing scope and severity of these emerging threats.

Following the Hugging Face breach, OpenAI has implemented comprehensive new security measures. The company is now enforcing stricter model monitoring throughout the development pipeline and placing greater emphasis on alignment and security during the post-training phase. These steps reveal just how widespread security vulnerabilities can be during the training and deployment stages of AI models.

Bluesky faces structural infrastructure challenges of its own. The platform has endured multiple DDoS attacks throughout the year, each causing significant service disruptions. These assaults reflect growing pressure targeting social networks.

The incidents experienced by both OpenAI and Bluesky demonstrate how critical security has become for both artificial intelligence systems and digital infrastructure as a whole.
