The pace of scientific research is accelerated. A new artificial intelligence framework called La Agente Optima is preparing to reveal the full potential of self-managed laboratories (SDL). This frame, traditionally, works of human experts—to identify goals, design experiments and adapt to results—automaticize.

The laboratories that govern itself already show assets but face serious limitations:

- Need human intervention: Laboratory managers were reviewed and making decisions in every step Tags
- The problem of adapting to changing conditions: When the experimental environment changes, campaigns can become ineffective
- Closed loop design: Available systems work in predefined limits
- Scale frame: difficulty to manage complex scientific targets automatically

La Agente Optima solved these challenges using agencyal AI (agentic AI). Frame can configure and control Bayesian optimization campaigns without human intervention. Artificial intelligence agent has understood scientific lenses, transformed them into experimental tasks that can work and revise their decisions when changing the environment.

This development allows researchers to significantly increase the speed of discovery, as well as separate the human ability into more strategic and creative tasks. Automating reduces cost and improves the quality of experimental data.
