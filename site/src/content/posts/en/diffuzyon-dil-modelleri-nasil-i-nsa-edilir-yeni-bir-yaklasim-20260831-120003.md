While transformer models in the field of artificial intelligence are dominated by text production for a long time, a new approach attracts attention. Diffusion models have been successfully applied in image processing, this technique is now used for language models. This method offered by Kuleshov Group brings a different perspective in the process of text creation.

Basic features of Diffusion-based language models are as follows:

- Application of noise reduction process (denoising) text production
- Gradual ability to create more meaningful texts
- Potential efficiency advantages compared to traditional transformer models  
- Ability to install different balances between text quality and production speed
- Providing quick interest (inference) by offering Parallelization opportunities

This new methodology provides flexible control during the training and removal of language models. Diffusion approach can learn to create consistent texts with step-by-step from random noise. In this way, researchers can get more control over balance between text quality and calculation cost.

Kuleshov Group’s blog post explains the practical implementation of diffffusion language models in detail, and this technology offers potential advantages to GPT-like models. The academic and industrial research community shows that this approach started to question the dominant position of the transformer architecture.
