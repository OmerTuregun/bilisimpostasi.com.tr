A researcher has made an interesting contribution to cryptographic history by factoring RSA keys of an old Certificate Authority that remains from mid 1990s. This success is a proof of the evolution of modern encryption standards and what security clears can be serious.

The security of RSA encryption is based on mathematical difficulty degree of separating large numbers of asal multiplier. In the state of technology development, old keys are designed considering the time calculation forces:

- The keys of the former Certificate Authority to 1990s have become resolved with modern computer powers
- RSA factorization has become practical with today's high performance processor and algorithms Tags
- This event emphasizes the necessity of updating old crypto systems of organizations Tags
- Historical cryptographic data was now open to retrospective attacks Tags

This study is academically important because the foundation of digital certification chain shows the long-term security limits of the RSA system. The researcher states that the dissolving of old keys is not directly threatened, but the history traffic is potentially at risk.

These findings encourage organizations to regularly review encryption standards and migrate to strong key lengths. Cybersecurity strategies are now not only today, but also in case of taking into account future threats.
