Eric Wu, founder of Opendoor, can bring a practical solution to one of the biggest problems in the construction industry. NavigateAI offers a platform that provides real-time, hands free guidance** to construction workers through artificial intelligence-supported copilots after exit from stealth mode in May.

The company allows workers to take step-by-step instructions through smart phones and Meta's AR glasses. This technology aims to alleviate serious labor shortage in the construction field:

- Data center projects need workers on scale, such as requiring 4,000-5,000 workers per single
- NavigateAI can reduce the impact of missing staff issues by increasing workers' efficiency and accuracy
- Technology minimalizes errors by offering guidance on training or repetitive tasks
- Free design of hands makes workers work safer and focused

The company received $25 million in total from Elad Gil, Khosla Ventures and Lennar. This investment reflects increasing interest in artificial intelligence solutions in the construction industry. Wu’s Opendoor experience offers a solid history in building scalable technology platforms.

The success of NavigateAI can be a critical milestone in the digitalization and automation journey of the construction industry. The equipment of construction workers with modern technology tools is the potential to complete both projects on time and improve labor safety.
