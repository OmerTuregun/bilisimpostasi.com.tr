Huawei carries a slightly more forward to folding phone category with Mate XT 2, which offers in Chinese market. The new device adopts the privacy-oriented display technology located in Galaxy S26 Ultra, which Samsung released this year, and combines it with three-story design.

Featured features regarding Huawei’s new model:

-**: Adopts imported folding mechanism used in Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold, which offers a more compact closed situation.
-**Privacy screen technology**: As the first digit phone, Samsung integrates the privacy technology we see in S26 Ultra, allowing the display to be powered in one direction.
- **Available hardware**: Unlike the first Mate XT, the ascorbic style leaves the folding mechanism and prefers to adopt a more modern approach.

Original Mate XT was launched in China at the end of 2024, then reached international markets a few months later. The new generation is inspired by the design strategy of Samsung** and increases the competition in the phone market. The privacy screen technology offers an important advantage especially for those who use phone in public areas, and this feature will come to the phones that can be folded, meet the expectations of the sector. This move of Huawei shows Samsung to strengthen its own technology infrastructure while following innovations.
