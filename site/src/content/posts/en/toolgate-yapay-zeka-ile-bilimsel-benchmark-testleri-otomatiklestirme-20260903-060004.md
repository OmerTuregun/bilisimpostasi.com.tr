The creation of scientific benchmark tests needs manual operation of field experts for many years. But a new research released in arXiv shows that this process is largely possible to automate. The system called ToolGate makes it easy to build scientific benchmark using the ability of language models to recommend fast questions.

Experts in traditional methods follow these steps:

- Textbooks, articles published and adaptation content from online sources
- Providing accuracy by controlling the work of each individual
- Significant labor expenditure per item to create high quality assessments
- Prepare accurate and testable answers for questions

The innovative approach of ToolGate is based on the idea to quickly produce the question candidates of language models and then pass through a executable admission line. The system focuses on scientific questions that can be verified specifically automatically, which greatly reduces the manual control work.

This development shows how artificial intelligence can turn scientific research infrastructure. Benchmark tests are critical to measuring the progress of machine learning models. Systems like ToolGate increase production speed while maintaining high quality standards.

Research results reveal how AI-supported approaches can be used to create valuable and reliable assessments in the science world.
