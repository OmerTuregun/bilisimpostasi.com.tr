To turn academic articles into presentation is a much more complex process than a single automatic step. ConvDeck is a new artificial intelligence system designed to solve this difficulty, pusherative improvement is centered and strengthens dialogue between users and the system.

The system uses three basic structures:

- The architectural, which works with stage-based user feedback, divides the presentation process into controlled steps and provides quality rise in each cycle
- Multi-media (multi-agent) combines systems with external user contribution by developing internal criticism-revision cycles
- The speaking interface allows users to interfere with slide decks (deck) natural language

While traditional automatic conversion tools offer static output, ConvDeck adopt a different approach. The system actively takes the user's opinion at every stage of the presentation (the choice of header, content editing, visual element placement). In this way, presentations become suitable for listener, more than just summarize the article.

The essence of the research is to design an open, transparent and user-oriented process instead of closed loops. Academicians, researchers and professionals will now spend less manual labor to achieve high quality presentations from the article content. This development represents an important step in the publication of academic communication and science.
