The presence of artificial intelligence models on the internet is rapidly expanding. According to a newly published research, ChatGPT has tips that the web pages published since the launch date are written by artificial intelligence in about three of them. This finding shows how deep impact AI technology has in the production of content.

- ChatGPT and similar AI models began to play a central role in writing and editing new content published on the web Tags
- The research had used analytical methods that defines texts that have been written or significantly contributed by AI Tags
- A large part of the Internet has now been filled with content produced by the machine instead of human, search engines and user behaviors Tags
- This trend revealed new questions about original content creation, copyright and information accuracy Tags

The role of AI in content production is beyond simple text writing. Companies integrate AI tools in many areas from news sites to e-commerce platforms. This situation increases productivity in the digital ecosystem and also brings quality and authenticity concerns.

Adopting advanced technology and fast shows that there is a rooted change in the internet structure. After that, the role of artificial intelligence plays in the digital world will be more pronounced and adapt to this change will become critical for both content producers and users.
