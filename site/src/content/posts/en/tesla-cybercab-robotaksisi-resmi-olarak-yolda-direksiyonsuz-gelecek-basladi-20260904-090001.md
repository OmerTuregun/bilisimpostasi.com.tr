Tesla began to serve as an official service of his self- anticipated step by jumping **Cybercab**. The vehicle with advanced technology, minimalist design and full autonomy has searched the door of a new era in autonomous transportation industry. This vehicle, which does not have a steering wheel, fully trusts artificial intelligence and sensor technology, delivers passengers to their targets.

The main features of Cybercab are:

- Steeringless design: There is no steering mechanism for human control on the vehicle; all operations are automatic
- Full autonomy: Sensors and AI networks are real-time decision by analyzing traffic
- Passenger-oriented interior design: an independent, comfortable and modern cab from traditional car concept
- Electric power: Electric motor with environmental friendly and operational costs low  u
- Scalable service: a optimized robot taxi solution for urban transportation

This step is an important milestone in the implementation of autonomous vehicle technology to the real world. Tesla offers its own driving software for many years as a **somut service** in this project. Not only technology display, it provides a system that works for real users.

Cybercab has started to answer important questions about the future of urban transportation. Although security continues legal regulations and general acceptance processes, this launch shows that autonomous transportation is possible in practical life. Tesla predicts that this move will promote other automotive and technology companies to develop similar projects.
