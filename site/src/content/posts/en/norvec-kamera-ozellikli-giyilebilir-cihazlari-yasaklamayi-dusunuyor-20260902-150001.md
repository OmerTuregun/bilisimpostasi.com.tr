Norway evaluates bringing strict regulations on wearable technologies with camera function. The country stated that such devices create serious risk for **history violations** and started their work on potential prohibition.

The main causes of this initiative of Norway are:

- Smart glasses with camera can enable video recording in environments where the user is not aware
- High risk of uncontrolled image record because devices are not light and visible
- Monitoring of individuals in public areas, workplaces and private spaces without consent is possible
- The existing data protection laws can not fully meet this rapid developing technology

Norway aims to create a suitable arrangement framework rather than completely ban technology. This step reflects increased worries for privacy rights in Europe. Other European countries also have similar concerns and think that wearable camera devices should be controlled.

This decision shows the increased sensitivity of **topimmun in AI-supported surveillance technologies**. Norway’s approach can be an important reference point for balance between advanced technologies and fundamental human rights.
