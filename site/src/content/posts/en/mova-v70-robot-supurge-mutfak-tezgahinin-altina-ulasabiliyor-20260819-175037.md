While most robot vacuums face limitations with environmental obstacles like stairs, they encounter another persistent problem: accessing the space beneath large furniture. The Mova V70 Ultra tackles this challenge through an innovative mechanical arm design.

**Design Innovation**

Unlike conventional disc-shaped robot vacuums, the Mova V70 Ultra features a movable arm that allows it to navigate over obstacles taller than its standard profile. By reaching under kitchen tables, dining tables, and similar furniture, it collects dust and debris that conventional robot vacuums typically leave behind.

**Practical Application**

Kitchen and dining areas represent some of the home's most challenging cleaning spaces. Bread crumbs and food debris accumulate regularly in these zones. The Mova V70 Ultra's design provides direct access to these problem areas, ensuring thorough cleanliness.

**Cleaning Performance**

The device combines both sweeping and mopping capabilities, enhancing its versatility. The arm mechanism expands the range of cleaning functions available to users.

**Home Automation Trend**

This product exemplifies the ongoing evolution of household robots toward greater intelligence and multifunctionality. Designers have engineered solutions to real-world challenges users face daily, demonstrating that robotic products are increasingly developed with user needs at their core.
