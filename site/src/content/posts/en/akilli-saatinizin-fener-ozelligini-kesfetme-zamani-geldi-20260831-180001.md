Smart watches offer more functionality and many users have not yet discovered the most useful features of these devices. Specifically ** included lantern feature** reveals as a much more practical tool than expected in daily life. While popular manufacturers like Garmin have offered this feature in leading models, other brands also developed similar solutions.

The main advantages of lantern feature on smart watches are:

- Providing enlightenment in dark environments by keeping hands empty
- Quick access to your head in case of emergency
- acquiring an additional light source without consuming the phone battery
- Practical use during sports and outdoor activities m
- A solution for elderly and disabled persons

This function is especially useful for camping, hiking and night sports activities. LED technology placed on the wrist offers an ergonomic experience than phone flashlight. The brightness level that Garmin models provide is enough to meet most daily needs.

If you ignore the hidden features of your smart watches, it is worth checking the lantern option especially. This simple but effective tool can make your life easier and grow in imdade in unexpected moments.
