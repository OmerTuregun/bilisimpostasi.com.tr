Face recognition technology brings serious privacy risks. A recent breach, these concerns suffered from the eyes: a reverse call service, accidental protection of the database of millions of people with a hundred photos. This event reveals how sensitive the biometric data is and the obligations that companies need to take responsibility.

The main points of violation:

- **Milyonsca photos** found in the internet in no encrypted access
- Reverse search service allows users to identify individuals by uploading a photo
- Data from the angle contains biometric information that can be used to train facial recognition systems
- Violation seems to be welded from data configuration error
- Security researchers reported the problem and reported to relevant parties

Such violations show how large risks are in the infrastructure of services based on AI and biometric technology. Face recognition databases can be abused by malicious users for surveillance, identity hunting or other illegal activities. **The company's data security standards** has been seriously upgraded.

Event emphasizes that editing institutions and technology sector should create stricter rules for biometric data protection. Users should also be more careful to upload personal photos to such services.
