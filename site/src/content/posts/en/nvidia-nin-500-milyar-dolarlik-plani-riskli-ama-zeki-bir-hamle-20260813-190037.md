Nvidia is developing a new strategy to prevent its GPUs from losing value. The company aims to persuade financial institutions to invest in AI infrastructure, ensuring that lenders provide continuous support for its products. While this plan represents a clever long-term approach to preserving hardware value in the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence market, it carries significant financial risks against potential market fluctuations.

Nvidia's move demonstrates how crucial it has become for hardware manufacturers in the technology industry to create entire financial ecosystems, rather than simply selling products.
