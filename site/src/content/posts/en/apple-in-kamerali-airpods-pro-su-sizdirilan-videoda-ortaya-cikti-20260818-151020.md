Apple's planned camera-equipped AirPods Pro 3 has appeared in a demo video found within macOS Tahoe 26.7 Release Candidate. The footage, discovered by MacRumors, showcases the device's capabilities by demonstrating how a user can point the built-in camera at a book cover, with the Visual Intelligence feature identifying the title.

The demo, narrated by Siri, is designed to highlight the new hardware's visual recognition abilities. The device design resembles a bulkier version of Apple's current AirPods Pro 3 model. This development reveals how Apple is approaching the integration of camera technology into wearable devices.
