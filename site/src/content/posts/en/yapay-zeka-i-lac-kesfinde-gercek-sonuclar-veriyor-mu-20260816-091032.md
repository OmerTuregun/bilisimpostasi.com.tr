While artificial intelligence promises to revolutionize drug discovery, the question remains: are real-world results meeting expectations? The scientific community is beginning to provide answers.

## High Expectations vs. Practical Reality

Over the past few years, AI-powered drug discovery methods have attracted massive investment. Companies and research institutions believed that machine learning and deep learning would accelerate the treatment development process. However, in practice, significant gaps have emerged between initial optimism and actual results.

## Partial Successes

AI has proven successful in specific tasks. There are impressive results in predicting protein structures, modeling biological activity, and screening potential candidates. However, this does not translate to near-complete automation of the entire drug development pipeline from start to finish.

## System Complexity

Drug development is more than just chemistry. Regulatory requirements, clinical trials, adverse effect analysis, and the complexity of human biology present challenges that artificial intelligence has yet to fully overcome.

## Conclusion: Progress Exists, But Without the Hype

AI is an incredibly useful tool in drug discovery. However, it is not a complete solution to medical challenges. Working with realistic expectations—free from hype—will allow us to extract the best results from this technology.
