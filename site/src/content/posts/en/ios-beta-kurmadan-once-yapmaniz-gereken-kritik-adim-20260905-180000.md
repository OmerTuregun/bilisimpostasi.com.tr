before an exciting experience for those who want to test iOS beta, it is necessary to skip a rather important step. While it’s attractive to discover new features and improvements, it should be the first priority to protect your people. According to Engadget’s warning, many users miss this critical step review.

The risks of installing beta software are:

- Installing a system that is not yet stable can lead to instability and errors
- Applications can be closed unexpectedly or lost data
- You may have to return to the previous version of the system and delete the data at this time
- There may be no security open yet fixed in Beta versions
- The device may come to the state that does not work completely and you may need to take format

* *Activation is not only a standard iCloud backup.** The critical step proposed by Engadget is to create the archive backup of your iPhone. This method creates a full copy of your device and allows you to restore it seamlessly if necessary. Thus, if you regret beta experience, you can turn to your old state with all your data.

The new features you’re wondering to install the beta operating system offer immediate trial opportunity. However, this opportunity should not be balanced with safety sensitivity. A few minutes of preparation can protect you from data losses that may arise for months.
