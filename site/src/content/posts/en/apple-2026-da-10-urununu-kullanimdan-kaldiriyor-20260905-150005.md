Apple constantly updates its technology products, in 2026, the major number of old devices and software support are terminated. Although operating system updates offer new generation of smart watches and imaging technologies, this progress leaves some generations of products behind.

The products removed according to the company are:

- Finishing software support for old generations of iPhone and iPad series
- Cutting updates for old models of Apple Watch
- Finishing the production and support process of certain iMac and MacBook versions Home
- The introduction of old generation of iPad Air and iPad Pro from Apple details
- Removing some of the features for external monitors

This step is a typical example of Apple’s aggressive product update policy. The company removes old devices from support with new hardware and software versions every year. For users, this means they can be challenged at least four or five years to keep their previous devices up-to-date.

These changes in 2026 show that Apple’s new AI features and advanced hardware architectures are old technology to offer. This way of product life cycle management encourages consumers to regularly upgrade.
