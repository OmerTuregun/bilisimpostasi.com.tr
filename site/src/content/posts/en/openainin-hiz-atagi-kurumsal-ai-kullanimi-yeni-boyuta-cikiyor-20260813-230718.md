## OpenAI's Speed Revolution: Ultrafast Mode

OpenAI is rolling out a new high-speed variant of its GPT-5.6 Sol model designed to capture enterprise customers. The "Ultrafast" mode operates **14 times faster** than standard versions, significantly reducing latency in large-scale corporate applications and enabling real-time AI integration. This move demonstrates OpenAI's commitment to prioritizing both performance and speed alongside quality in the enterprise market, intensifying competition in the sector.

## How Organizations Are Using ChatGPT

New research published by OpenAI reveals detailed insights into how organizations are adopting ChatGPT across their operations. The study demonstrates the rapid pace at which AI technology is being integrated into business workflows and the measurable value it creates across various enterprise processes. These findings help companies make more informed decisions about their AI investments and better understand the speed of industry transformation.
