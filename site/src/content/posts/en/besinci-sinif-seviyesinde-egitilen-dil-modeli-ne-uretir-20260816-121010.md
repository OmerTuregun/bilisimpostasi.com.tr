## Performance of Language Models Trained on Limited Data

Artificial intelligence research is making an important stride in understanding how the quality and scope of training data shape language models (LLMs). This project tackles an intriguing experimental question: **What happens if a language model never progresses beyond fifth-grade level comprehension?**

### Research Objective

The diversity and depth of training datasets play a critical role in developing language models. This study deliberately used limited and simplified text data to observe what types of constraints would emerge in the model's capabilities and performance.

### Expected Findings

Such a model would likely exhibit:
- **Limited vocabulary**
- **Difficulty understanding complex concepts**
- **Failure in tasks requiring abstract thinking**
- **Simpler responses in grammar and spelling conventions**

### The Importance of AI Training

This research emphasizes a fundamental truth in machine learning: language models cannot exceed the knowledge and concepts present in their training data. A model's capabilities are directly constrained by the scope and complexity of the text material it has been exposed to.

The study highlights how critical **data quality**, **diversity**, and **depth** are in developing artificial intelligence systems, while also providing valuable evidence of how dependent language models are on their training environment.
