An important step in solar energy technology is assigned. Solar Window company developed a revolutionary solution for architectural structures: self-adhesive solar film, which is only 0.85 millimeter thickness. This innovation offers a completely different approach from the structure that requires weight and installation of traditional solar panels.

The basic features of this technology can be summarized as follows:

- **Ultra fine design** can be glued directly to glass surfaces and minimize aesthetic concerns
- Professional installation does not require due to its self-adhesive feature, reduces cost
- Can be applied to various architectural elements including slope and flat surfaces with flexible structure
- Scalable to existing window systems, can be integrated into new construction
- Energy production potential can accelerate the expansion of the building-integrated photovoltaic (BIPV) solution

This development opens the opportunity to get energy from building exterior coatings and windows. Large glass surfaces used in city architecture can now be transformed into active energy production tools by being a passive element. When applied in commercial and residential buildings, carbon footprint is predicted to significantly reduce.

This innovation of Solar Window offers practicality and scalability for energy conversion technologies. In the face of global energy demands, such integrated solutions can become standard in the construction sector in the near future.
