Roku announced a new move in television market **first OLED TV series**ni. This step is evaluated as part of the strategy for the company’s premium screen technologies. New models will be available on Amazon as of this fall.

- OLED technology shows black colors in real sense by lighting on pixel basis
- Pricing starts from 999 dollars, entry level is a competitive range for premium segment
- Amazon plays an important role in Roku’s distribution strategy by offering the first access to the promotion
- OLED panels offer lower combustion risk and better contrast rates

Roku’s introduction to OLED TV market means that the company combines traditional smart TV operating system with ultra-high-quality display hardware. This integration will provide users with premium image quality and Roku’s familiar software experience. The potential to provide price advantage to other premium TV manufacturers is also an important factor.

With increasing OLED demand and technology costs in the market, Roku is expected to enter this segment of companies such as Roku. These new products that will be presented in the market during the autumn period can create an important change in the middle and upper segment TV market.
