HTC opens access to western users at the end of the expected Vive Eagle smart glasses for a long time. This device equipped with integrated cameras aims to carry the increased reality (AR) experience into everyday life. Introduction to Western market shows serious intention in this field of technology company.

The main features of Vive Eagle:

- **Embedded cameras** can scan the environment in real time and support AR applications
- **Available markets**: opening official sales channels in USA, Europe and Australia
-**Weight and design**: Compact structure suitable for daily use
- **Software support**: Compatibility with applications in the HTC ecosystem
-**Price category**: Premium segment positioning between smart glasses

Vire Eagle is the answer of HTC against Meta Ray-Ban and other competitive products. The camera functionality of glasses opens doors to applications such as social media integration and quick access to information. The Western market opening is a sign to become the main current of AR technology.

Even though not yet full pricing and all specifications are announced to the public, this move of HTC is expected to be an important step in the AR ecosystem.
