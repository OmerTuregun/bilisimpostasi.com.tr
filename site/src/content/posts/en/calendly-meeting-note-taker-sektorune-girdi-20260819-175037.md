Calendly has developed its own AI assistant to extend its reach beyond meeting scheduling software. The new service, called Callie, adds meeting note-taking and assistant capabilities to the platform.

**A Booming Market Opportunity**

The meeting note-taking and assistant market has experienced explosive growth in recent months. Solutions from OpenAI's Grok, Otter.ai, Microsoft Copilot, and numerous others now compete in this space. Calendly's entry into the market underscores how AI integration has become essential for meeting management tools.

**What Callie Can Do**

Callie automatically captures notes during meetings, summarizes key discussion points, and identifies action items. This frees users from the distraction of manual note-taking, allowing them to participate fully in conversations.

**The Integration Advantage**

With millions of users already relying on Calendly for scheduling, the platform is positioned to offer seamless integration of AI note-taking. The combination creates a streamlined workflow: plan your meetings and have them automatically documented—all in one place.

**AI Becomes Standard**

This move reflects how deeply artificial intelligence is now embedded in office and productivity software. Every major software company is either developing its own AI assistant or integrating one into existing products.
