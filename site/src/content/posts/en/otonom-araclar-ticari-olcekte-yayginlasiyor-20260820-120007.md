Autonomous vehicle technology continues advancing toward mass-market commercial deployment. Waymo is expanding its cost-effective next-generation Ojai robotaxi to all riders across three cities, demonstrating that autonomous transportation has become economically viable.

The Ojai design reflects Waymo's commitment to prioritizing cost efficiency in pursuit of profitability. This strategic move:

- Expands robotaxi services to a broader demographic of riders
- Enables testing of operational economies of scale
- Validates autonomous vehicles as a legitimate, sustainable business model

Waymo's expansion marks a significant milestone, moving autonomous transportation beyond theoretical concept into practical reality for millions of daily users.
