Anker continues to develop technology products aimed at improving sleep quality. The new Soundcore Sleep Earbuds 4 and 4 Pro series are designed to make your sleep seamless especially in the lateral soothe position. The company offers a more effective solution by making audio masking technology one step ahead in these new models.

Species that stand out of new headphones are:

- **Ses masking technology**: Sound masking technology in the last year A30 model is preserved, even able to effectively suppress the snoring voice of the spouse
- **Intuitive active noise blocking (ANC)**: Better work to reduce other noise in the environment
-**Smaller design**: 20 percent smaller communication area compared with A30 means less print on the ear
-**Long battery life**: significantly improved when compared to the previous generation Tags

Proper headphone selection for comfortable sleep is critical. During the lateral soothe headphones do not create excessive pressure in the ear; otherwise the quality of sleep decreases. Anker’s 20% smaller design is fully aimed at solving this problem.

Technology companies now invest seriously in sleeping products. The improved features of Sleep Earbuds 4 range shows that sleep technology is rapidly running. If you are experiencing noise problems in your bedroom or want to improve your sleep, these new models may be worth taking evaluation.
