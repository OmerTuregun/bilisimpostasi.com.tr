While artificial intelligence and technology sector develops rapidly, this area’s most basic needs are changing: **the presence of more scientists**. When people talk about reduction and optimization in technology industry, people are in the background of increasing the human power needed for scientific progress.

The effects of scientists are felt in various areas:

- The lack of qualified staff in AI research and development slows down the speed of projects
- Since research budgets in universities are limited, talented scientists are turning to the sector
- While basic scientific research is neglected, only profitable applications are first
- Graduates from the educational system are not enough to meet the expectations of the industry
- International scientists migration weakens the emerging countries

The progress speed of technology depends on the quality of basic scientific researches. In-depth scientific information is necessary in every field from climate solutions to biomedical innovations to quantum calculation of the ethical use of artificial intelligence. However, there are not enough experts to produce this information.

For the sustainable growth of the sector, the increase of investments in the socio-science research** and the need to promote academic careers. We need not only engineers, researchers, theories and laboratory employees. The future innovation will depend on whether we invest science today.
