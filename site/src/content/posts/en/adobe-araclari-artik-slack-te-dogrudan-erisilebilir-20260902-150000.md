The new partnership between Adobe and Slack allows users to access their favorite creative tools directly from the messaging platform. This integration aims to reduce time loss by making workflow centralized.

The scope of the integration contains:

- **Adobe Express** can be used in Slack for design and editing tasks
- **Adobe Premiere**, video editing operations can be done with real-time cooperation
- **Adobe Acrobat**, PDF management and signing can be performed directly from Slack
- Team members can work on common projects in Slack channels
- File sharing and feedback process become faster and organized

This approach eliminates the problem of context switching (link change) of creative teams. By taking advantage of Slack’s central communication center, Adobe tools are now available without getting away. Thus, designers, editors and other creative professionals can work more efficiently.

Integration refers to the increasing importance of inter-platform unity in modern business environment. Adobe and Slack gives an important message about the future design of productivity tools with this step.
