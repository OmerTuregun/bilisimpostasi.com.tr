In parallel with the rapid growth of artificial intelligence and cloud computing services, data center investments reach record levels. While Texas becomes the most important center of this digital infrastructure revolution, the interactive map prepared by KXAN visualizes data center project around 600 in the state.

This comprehensive data tracking system offers the following information:

- **600 units in operation and planned data center project** Placed in Texas
- Access to project details with verified location data by human
- Detailed analysis of the geographical distribution and economic effects of data centers
- Comparison of investment potential on city and region
- contextual information showing energy infrastructure, labor resources and regional advantages

The reasons why Texas stands out in terms of data center investments are clear: cheap electricity, large land, reliable energy sources and easy transportation. The tremendous processing power demands that artificial intelligence models need to be trained and operated have become the first choice of state tech giants. This trend means new employment opportunities and technological infrastructure development in the local economy.

Through the map, the public can access this information, provide transparency in regional planning and investment decisions. The positioning of Texas in digital economy seems that the country’s technology landskeypini will deepen in the next decade.
