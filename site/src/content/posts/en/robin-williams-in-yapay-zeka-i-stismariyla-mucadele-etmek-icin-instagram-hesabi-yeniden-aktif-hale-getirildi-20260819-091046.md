Zelda Williams, the daughter of the late actor Robin Williams, had previously spoken out about deepfake videos of her father being exploited online. Now, the Williams family is taking direct action to address the problem.

Zak, Zelda, and Cody Williams have taken over their father's Instagram account with the goal of transforming it into "a safe, trusted space that honors their father's legacy through authentic stories, photos, videos, and memories shared with care and warmth."

This move underscores the growing impact of artificial intelligence technology on cultural and personal legacies. Deepfakes and other AI-generated content are creating new challenges around controlling the online presence and legacy of deceased celebrities.

The Williams family's initiative represents an important approach to questions surrounding the digital rights and responsibilities of celebrities' families in managing their loved ones' digital legacies.
