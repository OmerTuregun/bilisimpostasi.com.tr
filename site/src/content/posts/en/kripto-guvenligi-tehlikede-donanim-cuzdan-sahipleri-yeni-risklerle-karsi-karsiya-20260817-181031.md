A new security threat has emerged in the cryptocurrency world. Data breaches at shipping companies used to deliver hardware wallets have compromised personal information, putting wallet owners at serious risk.

## What's the Problem?

Hardware wallets used to store cryptocurrency are typically shipped to customers via courier services. Recent data breaches have targeted these shipping companies' systems, exposing recipient information including addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses.

## What Are the Risks?

The stolen personal data enables attackers to pose several threats:

- **Physical Targeting**: Attackers now know the home addresses of wallet owners
- **Social Engineering**: Fraudulent attempts become more convincing with accurate personal details
- **Targeted Attacks**: Identification of potential wealthy targets who hold cryptocurrency assets

## How Can You Protect Yourself?

Cryptocurrency wallet owners should exercise extra caution during this period, approach incoming messages with suspicion, and enable two-factor authentication on all accounts.
