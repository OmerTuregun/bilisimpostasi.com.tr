A new player appears in the market of portable gaming devices. Anbernic offers its own reinterpretation for popular Nintendo Switch Lite console. This device called RG 55G1 will be officially available on 31 August.

The main features of the new device are:

- **With a price starting from 150 dollars**, it offers a convenient entry point
- Allows aesthetics to users in three different color options
- Nintendo Switch Lite is inspired by the design language but brings its original interpretation
- This model developed by Anbernic was designed to cater to users interested in alternative portable gaming platforms

Anbernic is a manufacturer operating for a long time on emulator devices and independent gaming platforms. The company RG series is widely accepted among gaming enthusiasts. This new model looks part of the company’s more mainstream market-oriented strategy.

RG 55G1’s market success will show you how much demand will be created with price point and color options. This move of Anbernic will be explained in the next month, which will affect the competition in the portable gaming console category.
