Acer is preparing to draw a new limit in the portable technology world. The new device called Project DualPlay Mini, offers the functions of a small laptop computer with gaming console in one package. This machine stands out as a solution designed for mobile players and frequent travelers.

The main features of DualPlay Mini:

- The compact design optimized to play the game offers flexible use with interchangeable controls
- structure that provides access to a full operating system and produactivity applications for operation mode
- Portable-oriented sizes, a practical form factor that you can carry in pocket
- Ability to switch smoothly between two modes of use
- enough processing power to run modern games smoothly

The device looks like made for users who want not to stay at home. In the morning you can play the game, you can check the e-mail at lunch hours, and the evening offers a experience that you can return to the game again. This kind of**hibrit solutions** see more demand and Acer can display the direction of this move.

This initiative of Acer is an important point about the future direction of portable technology. DualPlay Mini is trying to provide a device that supports both areas instead of compromising between gaming and efficiency.
