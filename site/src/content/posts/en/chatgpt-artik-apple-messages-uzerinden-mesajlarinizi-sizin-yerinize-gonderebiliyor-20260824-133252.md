Message writing work can now be done by artificial intelligence. Apple offers a new feature to significantly reduce users’ text writing load thanks to ChatGPT integration. This development is one of the most clear indicators that the mobile communication starts shaping the future.

ChatGPT provides the following advantages with Apple Messages plugin:

-**Automatic text creation**: Simply specify the topic or key points, ChatGPT creates full message
-**Time saving**: Fast post is provided especially in long or official messages
- **Natural language processing**: written messages look natural and fluent as written by human
– **Apple’s convenience in the ecosystem**: You don’t need to install additional apps thanks to Native integration
-**Customization options**: Users can control tone and style adjustments

This feature can be quite useful for people who have spent a lot of time to answer, or have difficulty in writing. But this development also brings questions about the naturality of personal communication. Although automated messages are practical, the ability to know who is written by the opposite party may affect the quality of social interaction.

This collaboration between Apple and OpenAI shows how deep integration of technology companies into our everyday life. Advancedly, such integrations can completely convert business and personal communication by becoming more common.
