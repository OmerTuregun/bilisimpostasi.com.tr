JBL is preparing to start a new era in audio technology. Cove series is evaluated as an important deviation in the history of the brand and represents a serious initiative in the market of all home audio systems. This three new Wi-Fi speaker is designed to ensure users live uninterrupted music experience in different rooms.

The main features of the Cove series are:

-**All home coverage area**: Three different speaker model works coordinate to bring high quality sound to every corner of the house
- Sync directly to the internet thanks to Wi-Fi connection and devices
- Flexibility in music stream: the possibility to feed different rooms with different music at the same time
- Simple installation and user-friendly interface
- Sound quality-oriented design reflects the well-known expertise of JBL

This move allows JBL to compete with competing brands like Sonos and Amazon Echo. The emergence of the Cove series will bring more competition in the category of home audio systems. Users can now create a compatible sound environment at home, using JBL's quality presentation.

Cove series shows that JBL is not only portable speakers, but also leader in fixed home audio solutions.
