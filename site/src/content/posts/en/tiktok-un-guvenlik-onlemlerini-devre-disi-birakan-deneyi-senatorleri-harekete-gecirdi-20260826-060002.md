TikTok’s content moderation system has moved to the US Senate movement in relation to an attempt that disables certain safeguards. The platform has closed the protection mechanisms designed to prevent users from overloading with harmful and disturbing contents for testing purposes, which measured how the application affects user engagement rates.

Basic findings arising from this experiment:

- TikTok, an AI and algorithm-based**filter system temporarily disables Tags
- The purpose of experience was to test whether the platform increases and increases its engagement metrics
- Senator refers to the risk of user safety of such experiments
- In particular, attention was taken to the risk of damaging young users and minors
- clearly emphasizes that the responsibility belongs to TikTok

The demand for Senators includes detailed information and descriptions about this experiment from the TikTok manager. The platform will show transparency on how safe the applications they do on behalf of improving user experience. According to industry observers, this incident has been observed once again with the efforts of enabling social media platforms to optimize snow-oriented.

This situation triggers new discussions on ethical implementation of artificial intelligence-based content control.
