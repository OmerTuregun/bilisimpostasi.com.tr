Authentication systems, the cornerstone of cybersecurity, are undergoing a fundamental transformation with passkey technology. This new approach is designed to address many of the problems associated with traditional passwords.

## Why Passwords Aren't Secure

Traditional passwords, used since the early days of the internet, remain an authentication method with significant vulnerabilities:
- Human error and weak password choices
- Susceptibility to phishing attacks
- Passwords can be compromised in data breaches
- Password management complexity
- Risk of password reuse across multiple sites

## Advantages of Passkey Technology

Passkeys solve these problems by using cryptographic keys:

**Technical Security:**
- Uses biometric recognition or device PIN
- Passwords are not stored on servers
- Natural resistance to phishing attacks
- Unique authentication for each site

**User Experience:**
- No need to remember passwords
- Faster login process
- Synchronization across different devices
- No need for password manager software

## The Transition Period

Complete migration to passkeys will take time. For now:
- Password managers remain essential
- Platforms supporting passkeys are growing steadily
- Windows, macOS, and mobile operating systems now offer passkey support
- Users can employ both methods simultaneously

This transition represents an important step in the evolution of digital security.
