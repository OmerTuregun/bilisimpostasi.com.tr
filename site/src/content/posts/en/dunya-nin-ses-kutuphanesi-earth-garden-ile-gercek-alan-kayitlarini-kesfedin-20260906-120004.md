Earth Garden opens a new dimension in online audio experience. The project makes real space audio recordings recorded from different locations in the world accessible via an interactive sphere. Users can simply listen to the unique sound environment of that region by clicking on a location on the map.

The platform offers the following features:

- **Various geographic resources**: Different environmental audio records from city noise to forest sounds, flowing water sounds  
- ** Interactive map interface**: Similarly to tour the planet, the possibility to switch between unlimited sites
- **Flexible use**: Background music, work/sleep environment or appropriate content for active listening
- **Cream inspiration from theRadio Garden**: Designed on the similar concept, focused on voice exploration m
- **Internal quality**: Professional records, small details (flower flowing dere sounds, city chaos) are convenient to highlight

Domain records (field Recordings) are one of the basic stones of ambient music and meditation applications. Earth Garden transforms this oil content into a universal discovery experience. When individuals want to hear silent music, they reflect the human party of technology that they use this platform when they want to remove curiosity or geography.

With its open design, Earth Garden offers the opportunity to build social connections in digital environment and experience cultural diversity through sound.
