Multi-materyal 3D printing technology allows the production of digital materials created from the controlled blends of solid and flexible materials. Mechanical properties of these materials vary a layer wider range compared to pure materials and exhibits complex, composition-dependent behavior. New research is an important step to solve this compelling material modeling using artificial intelligence.

The researchers reveal the failure of traditional son deformation viscoestik models and offer a new approach:

- Using data-focused methods **access-linked constitution models** automatically explore
- Hyperelastik and viscoestik behavior develop open models in terms of the structure forecast at the same time
- Identifying the mechanical properties of material mixture rates directly with mathematical formulations
- Rate-dependent dampener reactions to represent more accurately than classic models

This operation can speed up material selection during design and optimization stages. Engineers can adjust composition rates more effectively to achieve desired mechanical properties. Especially in sensitive applications such as medical devices, aesthetic applications and structural components, such data-oriented models will play a critical role.

This advance at the intersection of artificial intelligence and material science opens the door to make production methods smarter and efficient.
