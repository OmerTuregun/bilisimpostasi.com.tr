According to a Politico report, the U.S. House of Representatives' Office of Legislative Counsel is struggling to manage error-ridden bill drafts created by artificial intelligence systems. The influx of flawed documents has significantly increased the office's workload.

The root cause stems from AI models' inadequate performance in drafting legislation, a task requiring specialized legal and technical expertise. This situation highlights the practical risks of deploying immature AI technology in critical institutional functions, where accuracy and precision are essential for effective governance.
