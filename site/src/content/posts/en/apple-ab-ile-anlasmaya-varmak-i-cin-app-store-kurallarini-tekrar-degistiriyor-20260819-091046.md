Apple is making sweeping changes to its App Store policies once again to resolve ongoing disputes with the European Union. The company announced it will bring all app distributors under a single set of business terms.

The most significant element of these changes is a new 5% fee called the Core Technology Commission, applied to apps distributed outside the App Store. This represents Apple's effort to make alternative distribution methods more profitable.

The new rules will take effect on October 1st. Apple stated it is taking these steps to resolve "disagreements with the European Commission regarding business terms and alternative distribution."

These negotiations highlight important questions about how technology companies are regulated. The fact that Apple is forced to make such changes repeatedly demonstrates the EU's firm commitment to digital market regulation.
