Fairphone 6 Plus represents a long-awaited progress in the sustainable technology world. According to the assessment of The Verge, this phone provides good performance and camera quality, as opposed to previous generations, while offering materials and high repairability supplied from ethical sources.

Fairphone’s mission has always been worth appreciation:

-**Etic source supply:** Use material suitable for remote, fair trade principles from conflict zones  
-**High repairability:** Users are able to easily change parts, extending the life of the device
-**Middle segment performance:** Fairphone from the first time with strong processor and quality camera
- **Long software support:** Operating system updates of the device open source and long term

The older Fairphone models were making significant technological bouncers to achieve sustainability goal. Poor processors and bad cameras were forced to make unexpected preferences of ecological conscious users. Fairphone 6 Plus seems to have found this balance. The new generation devices are designed to spend their watches in the electronic waste stack, not in the pocket of users.

This phone shows that the technology industry can take responsibility seriously and not compromise performance. This approach in the middle segment market plays a leading role in many more manufacturers to follow.
