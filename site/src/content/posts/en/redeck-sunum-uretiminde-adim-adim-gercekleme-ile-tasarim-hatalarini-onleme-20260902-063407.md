Although it’s apparently simple to convert documents into slides, it has become a serious engineering problem for artificial intelligence systems. A new research called ReDeck offers a breakthrough solution on this challenge: visual control and correction mechanism instantly in every design step.

Current presentation agents typically follow a monolithic flow:
- Slide or presentation deck writing
- Visual design is made after processing
- Receive feedback only at the end
- Errors such as text stone, overlapping of items and cutting are noticed
- The correction process needs to start again

ReDeck breaks this paradig. The system provides instant visual feedback by rendering slides immediately after each design step. This approach is possible to detect and resolve local errors instantly. Keep from the selection of content can also be verified against visual reality, until placing sensitive space.

This step-level improvement method makes the process more efficient while significantly increasing the presentation production quality. It is seen as a revolutionary step in the automaticization of corporate documentation, academic reports and commercial presentations. This research published in arXiv shows that it is possible to create reliable slide without human intervention.
