There is a promise that has been said for a long time in the game world: it is expensive to play the game on a quality computer. However, the new BC-250 processor of AMD begins to question this bias. Can we install a full-function gaming computer with just 60 dollars budget? Apparently this is no longer just dreaming.

Let's look at the advantages of BC-250 processor:

-**Low price:** 60 dollar price point, cheaper than one part of traditional gaming sets
- **Adjustable performance:** enough processing power for casual players and indie games
-**Energy efficiency:** Low electricity consumption means less heat production
-**Input level flexibility:** Ideal entry point for novices and budget limited players

AMD is expected to create an important change in the market segment of this move. Although technology companies are focused on premium products for a long time, price-performance balance like BC-250 good products can be made possible to reach a wider player base.

Of course, it makes sense to think that 60 dollar computer can play AAA games in recent graphics. But esports headers can offer the basic performance needed to play indie games and old classics. This means to call the door to millions of people.

BC-250 can enter the world of gaming for players with budget restriction.
