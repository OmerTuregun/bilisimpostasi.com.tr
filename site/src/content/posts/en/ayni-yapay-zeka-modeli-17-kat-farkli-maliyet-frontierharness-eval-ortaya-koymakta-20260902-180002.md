During evaluation of artificial intelligence models, environment selection and configuration decisions can make radical difference in operating costs. New research released by FrontierHarness reveals this fact with stunning data. In the event that a single model operates in nine different evaluation environments, the cost per pass shows 17-storey variation.

This finding has critical importance in AI development and distribution processes:

- **9 harness (value environment)** tests against the same model using Tags
- **17x cost difference per single pass** measured Tags
- Proper choice of environment can save significant savings in operational expenses
- Developer teams may not be aware of this variation
- Standard evaluation applications need to be reviewed

Research shows that technical teams need not only accuracy and performance metrics while model assessment, but also systematic comparison of cost effectiveness. There may be significant economic difference between two environments that give the same result. These findings offer a new perspective in the allocation of budget planning and resources for institutions running AI especially in scale.

FrontierHarness is a step towards standardization and transparency of such assessments. This research shared in open source environment can help AI community get more efficient choices.
