Electricization efforts of automotive industry gain a new acceleration. Magna International invests 35 million dollars to Yuma Energy, following a aggressive strategy in India-based battery exchange technology. This move aims to reconcile the role of traditional auto suppliers in the electric vehicle ecosystem.

The main features of investment:

- Magna's total investment in Yuma Energy reached 87 million dollars
- Canadian company provides strategic control by increasing majority share in Yuma
- Battery exchange system aims to eliminate the problem of charging time
- India stands out as a geography where the electric vehicle market grows rapidly
- Technology can improve accessibility by reducing vehicle costs

This investment decision reflects the growing belief that the battery change system can be the future mobility solution. This technology of automotive supplier worldwide, such as Magna, points a turn point in the industry. Powerful investment potential and energy storage issues in India show that this technology can quickly expand in the country.

This step of Magna can also improve the flexibility of energy infrastructure while increasing the accessibility of electrical vehicles.
