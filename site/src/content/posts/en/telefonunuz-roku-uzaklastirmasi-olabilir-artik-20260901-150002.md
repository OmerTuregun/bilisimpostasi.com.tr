The problem of getting lost for millions of who have Roku devices is now thawed. The technology company offers the ability to convert your smartphone to a full removal device through its own official mobile app. The best side is that this service is completely free.

You can do using Roku mobile app:

- Opening and closing TV
- Adjust volume level
- Navigation between channels and applications
- guiding and selection in menus
- Finding content using search function

Easy to download and use the app. iOS and Android platforms are available and there is no need for any additional software installation. With your phone, your Roku device is enough to be connected to the same Wi-Fi network. The application does not leave any deficiency to provide all functions as it is in a standard removal.

This solution offers a practical alternative in cases where physical removal is lost, damage or battery problems. Roku users can now be kept in control hands.
