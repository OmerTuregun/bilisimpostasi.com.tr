Sony's legendary WH-1000XM4 headsets return six years later. The original model released in 2020 was at the beginning with its superior sound quality, competitive active noise cancellation (ANC) and compact design. But after withdrawal from the market due to the stock finish, Sony again introduces this classic model by XM4C.

In the competition that Sony and Bose had to lead in noise cancellation, each new model was offering better features than the previous one. XM4 was one of the peak points of this struggle and was highly appreciated by customers.

Features known about the new XM4C model:

- It carries the same visual design with original XM4; the difference is presented in white color option
- Compact form factor and portability are highlighted
- Active noise cancelled technology Tags
- The same standards are maintained in sound quality
- Reproduction was taken over the market’s demand

The reason behind this move of Sony is that the XM4’s cavity still feels on the headphone market. The combination of advanced technology and classic success, Sony offers the opportunity to return to its former customers. XM4C is a reexplor of a proven model of success in the premium headphone category.
