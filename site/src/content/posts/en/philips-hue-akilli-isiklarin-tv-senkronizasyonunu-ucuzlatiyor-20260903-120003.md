Philips Hue is a bold step for the prevalence of synchronising smart lighting systems with televiyon. The brand deducts price barrier with new product series aims to reach a wider consumer audience. The solutions introduced in IFA Berlin 2024 indicate a new era in entertainment lighting category.

What Philips Hue offers include:

- **Sync Box 4K** starts from 150 dollars of the model, saving up to 61 percent of the 8K version compared to 385 dollar price
- The first camera-based solution called Play Screen Sync** is listed only at $119.99 price
- Both products are released immediately after IFA
- Technology synchronizes colors on TV screen with smart lights in real time

Traditional entertainment synchronization systems were up outside the access of mainstream consumers because it is expensive and complex to technical installation. Camera-based Play Screen Sync minimizes the installation complex by solving this problem elegantly. Sync Box 4K offers a classic solution that works with HDMI connections.

Price drop is a part of Philips Hue’s smart home market democraticization strategy. With upcoming sales, entertainment lighting technology will now find the opportunity to enter many homes.
