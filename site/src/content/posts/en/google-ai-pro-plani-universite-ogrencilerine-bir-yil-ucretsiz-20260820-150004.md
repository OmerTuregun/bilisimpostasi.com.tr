Google has launched an initiative to promote artificial intelligence technology across the education sector. University students will now have the opportunity to explore AI tools at no cost.

This program provides young learners with early access to AI technologies, preparing them for the future of the industry. The AI Pro plan grants access to advanced features and tools that would normally require a paid subscription.

Fostering AI literacy in educational institutions is a critical step for the rapidly evolving technology sector. Google's educational contributions like this support the development of the next generation of tech professionals.
