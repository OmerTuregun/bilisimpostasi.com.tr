Dyson continues to expand the limits of home technology. This time also developed three-in-one toothbrush called CameraJet. By combining 500-bit devices, artificial intelligence and camera technology, the dental brushing experience is completely replaced.

The main features of CameraJet are:

- The oral screening and analysis of the built-in camera system
- Automatic water extraction (waterflossing) function thanks to cleaning dental breaks
- Shaping task by seeing the shower function of the device itself
- Providing special cleaning recommendations for artificial intelligence algorithm
- Premium structure quality and reflection of Dyson’s recognized design heritage

The device converts traditional toothbrush into an active personal health tool. Camera technology helps the user to develop more effective cleaning strategies by following the dental and gum status. Automatic water launch and rinse features significantly reduce manual efforts.

The price point of 500 dollars is placed in CameraJet's premium product category. This move of Dyson shows that smart home technology can expand to what areas. This product, a new step towards interest in dental health, artificial intelligence and automation, can be the pioneer of future generation personal care devices.
