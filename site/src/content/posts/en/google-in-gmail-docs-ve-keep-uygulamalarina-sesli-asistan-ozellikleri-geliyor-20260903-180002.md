Google is the way to add a new size in artificial intelligence power to the most popular productivity applications. The new audio assistant modes offered for Gmail, Docs and Keep are able to interact with these tools of users. These features are designed to facilitate life when you need to get quick notes on mobile environments or quickly.

The main advantages of new features are as follows:

- **Gmail Live** recovers you from doing manual search to find where the email is in your frombox
- **Docs Live** lets you write and edit with voice commands on the document
- **Keep Live** accelerates fast not receiving and note search processes via voice command
- The features inspired by the Gemini Live experience are bringing into real-time speaker and offering a natural and fluent interaction
- Provides access to information while you are busy on the go or two hands

This move of Google shows the speed of integration of artificial intelligence assistants to everyday productivity tools. Designed specifically for mobile users, these voice modes are an important step in increasing efficiency. Inspired by the success of Gemini Live, these features aim to make the user experience more natural and accessible.

Google’s presenting innovations in this kind, the AI-supported assistants now show that they become an integral part of everyday applications, not only independent tools.
