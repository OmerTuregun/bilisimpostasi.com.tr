Bluetooth, which is enabled by default on most smartphones, is a technology that users do not pay attention but create serious security open. According to Engadget’s news, Bluetooth can continuously open your phone to make it vulnerable to various threats.

The main risks of keeping Bluetooth always active are:

- **Free access**: malicious contacts can access your data by connecting to your device in the open Bluetooth signal field.
- **Man-in-the-middle attacks**: Saldurganlars can listen to communication between you and your matched devices.
- **Determining your stay**: It can be possible to identify your volume about via Bluetooth signal.
-**: Harmful software can spread to other devices through Bluetooth tree.
-**Battery consumption**: Continuous outdoor Bluetooth shortens battery life of your device.

Security experts recommend that you close Bluetooth when you don’t use or make your device “visible”. This simple step significantly reduces the risk of discovery by attackers that randomly scan your phone. This measure becomes more critical especially when you are in public areas.

Keeping Bluetooth enabled only when necessary to protect your phone’s safety is one of the basic steps of your digital privacy.
