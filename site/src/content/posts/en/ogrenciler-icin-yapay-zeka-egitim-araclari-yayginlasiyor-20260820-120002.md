Artificial intelligence adoption in education is accelerating rapidly. Google is rolling out new study tools integrated into its Search and Gemini platforms to support students in their academic work. The move signals Google's intent to compete directly with rivals like OpenAI for the student segment.

As part of this strategy, Google is offering students a free one-year subscription to its AI Pro plan. The complimentary access to this service—normally valued at $200—forms part of Google's broader strategy to expand its student user base and establish AI tools as a standard component of educational technology.

These initiatives demonstrate that artificial intelligence is assuming a central role in the education sector, with major tech companies investing heavily to shape this growing market.
